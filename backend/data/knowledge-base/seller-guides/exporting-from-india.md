# Exporting from India - Complete Seller Guide

## Overview

India is one of the world's fastest-growing exporters, with exports reaching $500 billion in 2025. This guide helps Indian sellers successfully export through EsyGlob platform.

## Why Export from India?

### India's Export Strengths
- **Cost Advantage**: Competitive labor and production costs
- **Quality Exports**: World-class products across sectors
- **Skilled Workforce**: 500+ million working-age population
- **Infrastructure**: Modern ports, logistics, SEZs, and shipping
- **Government Support**: Export incentives and PLI schemes
- **Digital India**: Strong technology ecosystem

### Key Statistics
- **Exports (2025)**: $500 billion+
- **Growth Rate**: 10%+ annually
- **Sectors**: Pharma, IT, Textiles, Auto, Chemicals
- **Top Markets**: USA, UAE, China, UK, Singapore
- **Exporting MSMEs**: 500,000+ companies

## Getting Started as Exporter

### Step 1: Business Registration
- **GST Registration**: Mandatory for all businesses
- **Company Registration**: Private limited, LLP, or sole proprietorship
- **Import Export Code (IEC)**: Required from DGFT
- **Bank Account**: Current account in authorized bank
- **RCMC Registration**: With relevant export promotion council

### Step 2: Product Readiness
- **Quality Standards**: Meet international specifications
- **Certifications**: Obtain export certifications
- **Product Testing**: Third-party labs (SGS, TUV)
- **Packaging**: Export-worthy packaging
- **Labeling**: Meet destination country requirements

### Step 3: Platform Setup
- **Create EsyGlob Account**: Seller profile
- **Complete Verification**: Business verification process
- **List Products**: High-quality images and descriptions
- **Set Pricing**: Competitive pricing in USD/EUR/GBP
- **Payment Terms**: Clear terms for buyers

### Step 4: Export Documentation
- **Commercial Invoice**: Detailed with all required fields
- **Packing List**: Item count, weight, dimensions
- **Shipping Bill**: Filed with customs
- **Bill of Lading/Air Waybill**: Transport document
- **Certificate of Origin**: Issued by chamber or DGFT
- **Insurance Certificate**: Covering transit
- **Export Declaration**: Required customs filing
- **GSP Certificate**: For preferential duties

## Export Documentation Process

### Documents Required for Each Shipment

| Document | Issued By | Purpose |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **Commercial Invoice** | Exporter | List goods, prices, terms |
| **Packing List** | Exporter | Details each package |
| **Bill of Lading** | Shipping line | Title to goods (sea) |
| **Air Waybill** | Airline | Transport document (air) |
| **Certificate of Origin** | Chamber/DGFT | Country of origin |
| **GSP Certificate** | DGFT | Preferential duty |
| **Shipping Bill** | Customs | Export clearance |
| **Insurance Certificate** | Insurer | Covers transit |
| **Export License** | DGFT | For restricted goods |
| **Health Certificate** | Agriculture/FDA | For food items |

### EDI System (Electronic Data Interchange)
- India uses fully digital customs clearance
- ICEGATE for online filing
- Faster clearance (2-24 hours)
- Reduced physical intervention
- Digital tracking of shipments

## Export Incentives and Benefits

### 1. RoDTEP (Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products)
- **Purpose**: Refund of embedded taxes
- **Rates**: 0.5% - 7% of FOB value
- **Products**: All exported products
- **Claim**: Online through customs
- **Benefits**: Reduces effective cost

### 2. RoSCTL (Rebate of State and Central Taxes and Levies)
- **Purpose**: Refund of state taxes
- **Applicable**: Textiles and apparel
- **Benefits**: Improved competitiveness

### 3. EPCG Scheme (Export Promotion Capital Goods)
- **Purpose**: Duty-free import of machinery
- **Condition**: Export commitment of 6x duty saved
- **Benefit**: Upgrade technology

### 4. Free Trade Agreements
- **India-ASEAN**: Preferential tariffs
- **India-Japan**: Duty reductions
- **India-South Korea**: Competitive advantage
- **India-GCC**: Under negotiation

### 5. Manufacturing Promotion
- **PLI Scheme**: Production Linked Incentive
- **Make in India**: Manufacturing promotion
- **SEZ/EOU Benefits**: Tax and duty exemptions

## Export Pricing Strategies

### Cost Calculation
- **Ex-Works Price**: Factory cost
- **FOB Price**: Ex-works + loading + export documentation
- **CIF Price**: FOB + freight + insurance
- **Landed Cost**: CIF + import duties + clearance

### Pricing Factors
- **Raw Material Costs**: Changes in input prices
- **Currency Fluctuation**: INR/USD, INR/EUR
- **Freight Costs**: Sea/air freight changes
- **Duty Changes**: Export incentives
- **Marketing Costs**: Platform fees, promotion

### Pricing Example
For a product with factory cost ₹500:

| Price Type | Formula | Amount (₹) |

| Ex-Works | ₹500 | ₹500 |

| + Ocean Freight | ₹70 | ₹640 |
| + Insurance | ₹20 | ₹660 |
| **CIF Price** | **₹660** | **₹660 (≈ $8.00)** |

## Payment Terms for Exporters

### Recommended Payment Methods
| Method | Risk | Cost | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| **Letter of Credit** | Low (bank guaranteed) | High (1-3%) | First-time buyers |
| **D/P (Documents against Payment)** | Medium | Medium | Established buyers |
| **T/T with Deposit** | Medium | Low | Partial advance |
| **Open Account** | High | Low | Long-term relationships |
| **Cash in Advance** | None | High | New buyers |

### Protections for Exporters
- **ECGC Insurance**: Export Credit Guarantee
- **Forfaiting**: Sell receivables at discount
- **Factoring**: Invoice financing
- **Bonding**: Bonds against payment risk
