# International Trade Terminology - Complete Glossary

## A

### Anti-Dumping Duty
A tariff imposed on imports priced below normal market value to protect domestic industries.

### ASEAN
Association of Southeast Asian Nations - 10-country economic bloc.

### AWB (Air Waybill)
Document issued by an airline serving as a receipt for goods and contract of carriage.

## B

### Bill of Lading (B/L)
Transport document issued by a shipping line, serving as receipt, contract of carriage, and title document.

### BIS
Bureau of Indian Standards - Indian standards body.

### B2B (Business-to-Business)
Commerce between businesses, not between business and consumer.

## C

### CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight)
Incoterm where seller pays for insurance and freight to destination port.

### CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)
EU policy to prevent carbon leakage by imposing carbon cost on imports.

### CBP
US Customs and Border Protection - US customs authority.

### CE Marking
European conformity marking for products entering EU market.

### Certificate of Origin
Document certifying product's country of manufacture.

### CPTPP
Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership.

### C-TPAT
Customs-Trade Partnership Against Terrorism - US security program.

## D

### DAP (Delivered at Place)
Incoterm where seller delivers goods to named place, buyer handles import clearance.

### DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)
Incoterm where seller pays all costs including duties and taxes.

### DGFT
Directorate General of Foreign Trade - Indian trade policy authority.

### D/P (Documents against Payment)
Payment method where payment is required before documents are released.

## E

### EAR
Export Administration Regulations - US export controls.

### ECCN
Export Control Classification Number - US export classification.

### ECHA
European Chemicals Agency - EU chemical regulation authority.

### EPCG
Export Promotion Capital Goods - Indian export incentive scheme.

### Escrow
Third party holding funds until transaction conditions are met.

### EXW (Ex Works)
Incoterm where buyer handles all logistics from seller's premises.

## F

### FCA (Free Carrier)
Incoterm where seller delivers goods to carrier at named place.

### FCC
Federal Communications Commission - US electronics regulator.

### FDA
Food and Drug Administration - US food and drug regulator.

### FEMA
Foreign Exchange Management Act - Indian foreign exchange law.

### FOB (Free on Board)
Incoterm where seller delivers goods on board vessel at named port.

### FTA (Free Trade Agreement)
Agreement between countries to reduce trade barriers.

## G

### GATS
General Agreement on Trade in Services - WTO services agreement.

### GATT
General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade - WTO goods agreement.

### GDPR
General Data Protection Regulation - EU data protection law.

### GOTS
Global Organic Textile Standard - Organic textile certification.

### GSP
Generalized System of Preferences - Tariff preferences for developing countries.

### GST
Goods and Services Tax - Indirect tax in India.

## H

### HS Code
Harmonized System Code - International product classification.

### HTSUS
Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States - US tariff classification.

## I

### IEC
Import Export Code - Indian importer/exporter registration.

### IGST
Integrated Goods and Services Tax - Tax on imports in India.

### Incoterms
International Commercial Terms - Standard trade terms.

### ISF
Importer Security Filing - US customs security filing.

### ISO
International Organization for Standardization - Global standards body.

### ITAR
International Traffic in Arms Regulations - US military export controls.

## L

### L/C (Letter of Credit)
Bank guarantee of payment in international trade.

### Landed Cost
Total cost including price, freight, duties, and logistics.

## M

### MFN (Most Favored Nation)
WTO principle requiring equal treatment for all members.

### MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)
Smallest quantity a supplier will produce or sell.

## N

### NAFTA
North American Free Trade Agreement (replaced by USMCA).

### NLR
No License Required - Export without license.

## O

### OFAC
Office of Foreign Assets Control - US sanctions authority.

## P

### PAN
Permanent Account Number - Indian tax identification.

### PLI
Production Linked Incentive - Indian manufacturing incentive scheme.

## R

### RCEP
Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership - Asia-Pacific trade agreement.

### REACH
Registration, Evaluation, Authorization of Chemicals - EU chemical regulation.

### RoDTEP
Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products - Indian export incentive.

### RoHS
Restriction of Hazardous Substances - EU electronics regulation.

### RoSCTL
Rebate of State and Central Taxes - Indian textile export incentive.

## S

### SAD
Single Administrative Document - EU customs declaration.

### SDN
Specially Designated Nationals - OFAC sanctions list.

### SEZ
Special Economic Zone - Designated area with special trade benefits.

## T

### T/T (Telegraphic Transfer)
Bank-to-bank wire transfer payment method.

### TBT
Technical Barriers to Trade - WTO agreement on technical standards.

### TRIPS
Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights - WTO IP agreement.

## U

### UL
Underwriters Laboratories - US product safety certification.

### USMCA
United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement - North American trade agreement.

### USTR
United States Trade Representative - US trade policy authority.

## V

### VAT
Value Added Tax - Consumption tax in many countries.

## W

### WEEE
Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment - EU e-waste regulation.

### WTO
World Trade Organization - International trade organization.
