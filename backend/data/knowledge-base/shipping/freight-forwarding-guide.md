# Freight Forwarding - Complete B2B Guide 2026

## Overview

Freight forwarders are essential partners in international trade. This guide covers everything B2B businesses need to know about freight forwarding services.

## What is a Freight Forwarder?

A freight forwarder is a logistics company that arranges the transportation of goods on behalf of shippers.

### Services Provided

| Service | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Shipping Arrangement** | Book cargo space |
| **Documentation** | Prepare shipping docs |
| **Customs Clearance** | Handle customs |
| **Insurance** | Arrange coverage |
| **Warehousing** | Storage services |
| **Consolidation** | Combine shipments |
| **Tracking** | Shipment monitoring |
| **Consulting** | Logistics advice |

## Why Use a Freight Forwarder?

### Benefits

✅ **Expertise**: Knowledge of shipping regulations
✅ **Network**: Established carrier relationships
✅ **Cost Savings**: Better rates through volume
✅ **Time Savings**: Handle documentation
✅ **Risk Management**: Insurance and compliance
✅ **Door-to-Door**: Complete service

### When Not to Use

❌ Small shipments (use courier instead)
❌ Simple shipments (direct with carrier)
❌ Frequent, same-route shipments

## Services in Detail

### 1. Shipping Arrangement

**Activities**:
- Book cargo space
- Negotiate rates
- Select carrier
- Arrange transport
- Coordinate pickup

### 2. Documentation

**Documents Prepared**:
- Bill of Lading/AWB
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Certificate of Origin
- Insurance Certificate
- Customs Declaration

### 3. Customs Clearance

**Activities**:
- Classify goods (HS code)
- Calculate duties
- Prepare customs forms
- Liaise with customs
- Pay duties (if agreed)

### 4. Consolidation

**Definition**: Combining multiple LCL shipments into FCL

**Benefits**:
- Lower cost per unit
- More frequent shipments
- Flexibility

**Best For**:
- Small shippers
- Regular shipments
- Various destinations

### 5. Warehousing

**Services**:
- Storage
- Inventory management
- Order fulfillment
- Cross-docking
- Distribution

## Freight Forwarder vs Customs Broker

### Comparison

| Aspect | Freight Forwarder | Customs Broker |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Primary Role** | Arrange shipping | Clear customs |
| **Documentation** | Shipping docs | Customs docs |
| **Regulation** | Transport law | Customs law |
| **Licensing** | Varies | Licensed |
| **Services** | End-to-end | Clearance-focused |

### Combined Services

Many freight forwarders are also licensed customs brokers.

## Costs and Fees

### Typical Fee Structure

| Fee Type | Description | Amount |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| **Freight Charges** | Actual shipping cost | Market rate |
| **Forwarding Fee** | Service charge | $50-200 |

| Cost | Description |
|------|-------------|

## Red Flags

### When to Be Concerned

⚠️ **Too Good to Be True**: Unusually low rates
⚠️ **Pressure**: Urging you to sign
⚠️ **No Contract**: No written agreement
⚠️ **No Insurance**: Not carrying liability insurance
⚠️ **Vague Answers**: Can't provide clear information
⚠️ **No References**: Can't provide client testimonials

### Warning Signs

- Poor communication
- Hidden charges
- No tracking information
- Delays without explanation
- Asking for payment in cash
