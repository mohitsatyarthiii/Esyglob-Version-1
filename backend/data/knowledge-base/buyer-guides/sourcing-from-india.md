# Sourcing from India - Complete Buyer Guide

## Overview

India is one of the world's fastest-growing manufacturing hubs, offering competitive advantages in cost, quality, and scale. This guide helps B2B buyers source effectively from India.

## India's Manufacturing Advantage

### Key Strengths
- **Cost Advantage**: 30-50% lower than Western countries
- **Skilled Workforce**: 500+ million working-age population
- **Quality Standards**: Global certification compliance
- **English Speaking**: Ease of communication
- **Growing Infrastructure**: Modern ports and logistics
- **IT Advantage**: Strong technology integration
- **Stable Democracy**: Long-term business reliability

### Key Statistics (2025)
- **GDP**: $3.2 trillion
- **Manufacturing Output**: $800 billion (growing 10% annually)
- **Exports**: $500 billion (2025)
- **Global Rank**: 5th largest economy
- **FDI**: $60 billion received annually

## Certification and Standards

### Required Certifications
- **BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards)** - Quality mark
- **AGMARK** - Agricultural products
- **FSSAI** - Food safety
- **ISO 9001, 14001** - Quality and environment
- **UL, CE, RoHS** - Export certifications
- **GST Registration** - Tax compliance

### Export Documentation
- **Certificate of Origin** (non-preferential or preferential)
- **GSP Certificate** (if applicable)
- **Export Invoice** with GST details
- **Shipping Bill** or Bill of Lading
- **Insurance Certificate**
- **Packing List**
- **Customs Declaration**

## Trade Agreements and Preferential Tariffs

### India's FTAs
- **India-ASEAN FTA**: Southeast Asian countries
- **India-Japan CEPA**: Comprehensive partnership
- **India-South Korea CEPA**: Comprehensive pact
- **India-Mercosur**: Latin America (partially)
- **India-Singapore CECA**: Trade in goods and services
- **India-Gulf Cooperation Council**: Under negotiation

### Government Incentives
- **Production Linked Incentive (PLI)**: Manufacturing subsidies
- **Make in India**: Manufacturing promotion
- **Export Promotion Capital Goods (EPCG)**: Duty-free imports
- **Merchandise Exports from India Scheme (MEIS)**: Export incentives
- **Service Exports from India Scheme (SEIS)**: Service export incentives

## Practical Sourcing Tips

### 1. Supplier Discovery
- Use **EsyGlob** and other B2B platforms
- Verified suppliers on platform
- Trade fairs and exhibitions
- Government export portals

### 2. Verify Suppliers
- Check business registration with Ministry of Corporate Affairs
- Verify GSTIN and IEC (Import Export Code)
- Request factory visit photos or virtual tour
- Check Export Import Data (through platforms)

### 3. Quality Checks
- Request samples (minimum 5 units)
- Test for specifications
- Use third-party inspection (SGS, Bureau Veritas)
- Check for certifications and claims

### 4. Negotiation Strategy
- Start with small quantities
- Discuss MOQ flexibility
- Request better payment terms
- Discuss volume discounts
- Factor in logistics costs

### 5. Logistics and Clearance
- Most exports via: **Mumbai Port, Chennai, Mundra, Kandla, JNPT**
- Air freight via: **Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai**
- Use reliable freight forwarder
- Factor in time: 15-45 days sea, 3-7 days air
