## Quality Standards

### Key Certifications

| Certification | Purpose | Products |
|---------------|---------|----------|
| **OEKO-TEX Standard 100** | Harmful substances | All textiles |
| **GOTS (Global Organic Textile)** | Organic certification | Organic textiles |
| **BCI (Better Cotton Initiative)** | Sustainable cotton | Cotton products |
| **GRS (Global Recycled Standard)** | Recycled content | Recycled materials |
| **Fair Trade** | Fair labor practices | All textiles |
| **BSCI** | Social compliance | Supply chain audit |

### Testing Standards

**Physical Testing**:
- Tensile strength
- Tear strength
- Abrasion resistance
- Pilling resistance
- Dimensional stability
- Color fastness

**Chemical Testing**:
- Formaldehyde content
- pH value
- Azo dye content
- Heavy metals
- Pesticide residues
