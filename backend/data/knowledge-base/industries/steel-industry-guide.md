### For Importers

✅ **Verify Suppliers**: Check production facilities
✅ **Request Samples**: Test quality before bulk
✅ **Get Test Certificates**: Mill certificates required
✅ **Use Third-Party QC**: Independent inspection
✅ **Understand Specifications**: Know grade requirements
✅ **Factor All Costs**: Price, freight, duties, insurance
✅ **Use Secure Payment**: L/C or EsyGlob Pay
