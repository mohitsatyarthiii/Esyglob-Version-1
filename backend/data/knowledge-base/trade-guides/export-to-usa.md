# Export to USA - Complete Guide 2026

## Overview

The United States is the world's largest consumer market, importing over $3 trillion in goods annually. This guide will help you navigate US import requirements and regulations.

## US Market Overview

- **Total Imports (2025)**: $3.2 trillion
- **Top Product Categories**: Machinery, Electronics, Vehicles, Oil, Pharmaceuticals
- **Top Trading Partners**: China, Mexico, Canada, Germany, Japan
- **Key Ports**: Los Angeles, Long Beach, New York/New Jersey, Savannah

## Entry Requirements

### Customs Entry Types

1. **Formal Entry**: For shipments valued over $2,500
2. **Informal Entry**: For shipments under $2,500
3. **Section 321 - de minimis**: For shipments under $800 (duty free)

### Required Documents

1. **Commercial Invoice** - Detailed description, value, quantity, weight
2. **Packing List** - Item count, weight, dimensions per package
3. **Bill of Lading** - Sea or Air Waybill
4. **Entry Summary (CBP Form 3461)** - Filed by customs broker
5. **HS Classification** - 10-digit HTSUS code
6. **Country of Origin Certificate** - For preferential trade programs

### Additional Documents Required

- **FDA Prior Notice**: For food, drugs, cosmetics (must file 24 hours before arrival)
- **FCC Certification**: For electronic devices
- **DOT Certification**: For vehicles and auto parts
- **EPA Certification**: For chemicals and pesticides
- **USDA Permit**: For agricultural products
- **FWS Permit**: For wildlife and plants

## Key Regulatory Agencies

### U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP)
- Controls the entry of all imports
- Collects customs duties
- Enforces laws and regulations

### Food and Drug Administration (FDA)
- Regulates food, drugs, cosmetics, medical devices
- Requires facility registration and prior notice
- Inspects 2-5% of entries

### Federal Communications Commission (FCC)
- Regulates electronic devices
- Requires compliance testing
- Must affix FCC logo on products

### Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)
- Regulates chemicals and pesticides
- Requires registration of certain substances
- Establishes emission standards

### Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)
- Sets safety standards for consumer products
- Bans unsafe products and issues recalls
- Requires testing by CPSC-approved labs

## Trade Programs and Agreements

### General System of Preferences (GSP)
- Duty-free access for developing countries
- Renewed through December 2030
- Covers 3,500+ products
- Must meet 35% value-added rule

### USMCA (formerly NAFTA)
- US-Mexico-Canada Agreement
- No duties on goods meeting rule of origin
- Certificate of Origin required

### Other Agreements
- US-Chile FTA
- US-Colombia FTA
- US-Israel FTA
- Caribbean Basin Initiative (CBI)
- African Growth and Opportunity Act (AGOA)

## US Tariffs and Duties

### Average Duty Rates
- **General**: 3.5% average (most favored nation rate)
- **Section 301 Tariffs** (China):
  - List 1: 25% (2018) - Machinery, medical devices
  - List 2: 25% (2018) - Electronics, plastics
  - List 3: 25% (2019) - Consumer goods, textiles
  - List 4: 15% (2025) - All other goods

### Special Duties
- **Anti-Dumping Duties**: Imposed on dumped goods
- **Countervailing Duties**: Imposed on subsidized goods
- **Section 232**: Steel (25%) and Aluminum (10%)
- **Section 201**: Solar panels, washing machines

## Customs Clearance Process

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Pre-Arrival**: Submit documents to customs broker
2. **Arrival**: Cargo arrives at US port
3. **Entry Filing**: Broker files entry with CBP
4. **CBP Review**: Risk assessment and targeting
5. **Payment**: Duties and fees paid to CBP
6. **Release**: Cargo released for delivery
7. **Post-Entry**: Record keeping and audits

### Timeframes
- **Pre-Arrival**: 7-14 days before arrival
- **Entry Filing**: Within 15 days of arrival
- **CBP Review**: 2-24 hours
- **Release**: Same day if no inspection
- **With Inspection**: 3-10 days

## Labeling and Packaging Requirements

### Mandatory Labels
1. **Country of Origin**: "Made in [Country]"
2. **Product Name**: Clear description
3. **Manufacturer Info**: Name and address
4. **Care Instructions**: For textiles/apparel
5. **Warning Labels**: For hazardous products
6. **Dimensions and Weight**: On shipping containers

### Special Labeling Requirements
- **Food**: Nutrition Facts panel, ingredient list
- **Cosmetics**: Ingredient list (INCI names), warnings
- **Electronics**: Voltage, FCC mark, WEEE symbol
- **Chemicals**: GHS pictograms, safety data sheet

### Packaging Best Practices
- Use export-worthy packaging (carton, pallet, container)
- Mark packages clearly with: Consignee name, PO number, Country of origin, Handling instructions
- For sea freight: Use waterproof packaging, Use wooden pallets treated (ISPM 15)
- For air freight: Consider weight and dimensions, Use appropriate packaging materials

## Common Mistakes and Prevention

### 1. Incorrect HS Classification
**Mistake**: Wrong HTSUS code leads to wrong duty payment.
**Solution**: Request binding ruling from CBP, use NAFTA or USMCA origin.

### 2. Missing FDA Prior Notice
**Mistake**: Shipment denied entry for no prior notice.
**Solution**: File 24 hours before arrival, ensure facility is registered.

### 3. Using Wrong Entry Type
**Mistake**: Failing to file formal entry.
**Solution**: Know your value, use formal entry for over $2,500.

### 4. Inadequate Labeling
**Mistake**: Missing Country of Origin or other labels.
**Solution**: Review CPSC and other agency guidelines before shipment.

### 5. Customs Bond Issues
**Mistake**: Not having continuous bond in place.
**Solution**: Purchase annual continuous bond for all shipments.

## Tips for Successful US Export

✅ **Work with Licensed Customs Broker**: Essential for compliance
✅ **Open Continuous Bond**: Covers all shipments for the year
✅ **Register with CBP**: For faster clearance (C-TPAT)
✅ **Use Importer of Record (IOR)**: Ensure proper representation
✅ **Classify Products Early**: Determine HTSUS code during design phase
✅ **Plan for FDA/USDA/CBP Inspections**: Build time into schedule
✅ **Understand Landed Cost**: Know your total cost before quoting
✅ **Monitor Trade Policies**: Tariffs change frequently
✅ **Build Compliance Into Process**: Use third-party testing labs
✅ **Maintain Records**: Keep all documents for 5 years
