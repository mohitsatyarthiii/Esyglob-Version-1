# Trade Finance Guide for B2B Businesses

## Overview

Trade finance is essential for international B2B transactions, providing financing, risk mitigation, and payment solutions. This guide covers all major trade finance instruments available on EsyGlob platform.

## What is Trade Finance?

Trade finance refers to financial instruments and products that facilitate international trade. It bridges the gap between the time a seller ships goods and the buyer receives and pays for them.

## Why Trade Finance Matters

- **Cash Flow Management**: Access working capital
- **Risk Mitigation**: Reduce payment and performance risks
- **Working Capital Optimization**: Don't tie up cash in inventory
- **Business Growth**: Scale operations with financing
- **Supplier Relationships**: Pay suppliers on time
- **Competitive Advantage**: Offer better terms to customers

## Key Trade Finance Instruments

### 1. Letter of Credit (L/C)

**Types of Letters of Credit**

| Type | Description | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Revocable** | Can be modified without buyer consent | Rarely used |
| **Irrevocable** | Cannot be changed without all parties | Most common, safest |
| **Confirmed** | Another bank guarantees payment | Higher risk countries |
| **Unconfirmed** | Only issuing bank's guarantee | Low risk countries |
| **Sight L/C** | Payment immediately on presentation | Faster settlement |
| **Usance L/C** | Payment at specific future date | Allows deferred payment |
| **Transferable L/C** | Can be transferred to another supplier | Middleman transactions |
| **Revolving L/C** | Multiple shipments under same L/C | Regular shipments |

**L/C Process**
1. Buyer and seller agree on terms
2. Buyer applies for L/C from issuing bank
3. Issuing bank sends L/C to advising bank
4. Seller receives L/C and reviews terms
5. Seller ships goods and presents documents
6. Documents verified by banks
7. Payment released to seller

**Documentation Required**
- Commercial invoice
- Bill of lading/air waybill
- Packing list
- Certificate of origin
- Insurance certificate
- Inspection certificate
- Any other specified documents

### 2. Bank Guarantees

**Types of Bank Guarantees**
- **Bid Bond**: Guarantees bid will not be withdrawn
- **Performance Guarantee**: Ensures contractual performance
- **Advance Payment Guarantee**: Protects buyer's advance payment
- **Standby L/C**: Serves as guarantee of payment

### 3. Trade Credit Insurance

**What It Covers**
- Non-payment risk
- Commercial risk (buyer default)
- Political risk (currency controls, war, embargoes)
- Medium and long-term transactions

**Provider Examples**
- Euler Hermes
- Aon
- Marsh
- Zurich

### 4. Forfaiting

- **Medium-term**: 1-10 years
- **Large amounts**: Usually €100,000+
- **International**: Cross-border transactions
- **No recourse**: Seller transfers all risk
- **Fixed rate**: Discounted receivables

### 5. Factoring

- **Short-term**: 30-180 days
- **Domestic and international**
- **Recourse**: Seller retains risk (option)
- **Non-recourse**: Factor assumes risk
- **Flexible**: Based on invoices

### 6. Supply Chain Finance

**Types**
- **Reverse Factoring**: Buyer-validated invoices
- **Dynamic Discounting**: Early payment discounts
- **Inventory Financing**: Financing against stock
- **Distribution Finance**: Financing distributors

## Documents in Trade Finance

### Commercial Documents
- **Commercial Invoice**: Core document with all details
- **Packing List**: Details of goods in each package
- **Certificate of Origin**: Country of manufacture

### Transport Documents
- **Bill of Lading**: Contract of carriage (sea)
- **Air Waybill**: Contract of carriage (air)
- **Multimodal Bill**: For multiple transport modes

### Insurance Documents
- **Insurance Policy**: Covers transit risks
- **Insurance Certificate**: Proof of coverage

### Financial Documents
- **Draft/Bill of Exchange**: Payment order
- **Promissory Note**: Written promise to pay

### Regulatory Documents
- **Customs Declaration**: Required for clearance
- **Inspection Certificate**: Quality confirmation
- **Phytosanitary Certificate**: For agricultural goods

## Common Mistakes

❌ **Not Understanding L/C Terms**: Detailed review required
❌ **Insufficient Documentation**: Must match L/C exactly
❌ **Using Wrong Incoterms**: Affects risk and costs
❌ **Ignoring Currency Risk**: Monitor exchange rates
❌ **No Dispute Resolution Plan**: Document everything
❌ **Overlooking Additional Costs**: Bank fees, amendments

## Best Practices

✅ **Plan Ahead**: Trade finance takes time
✅ **Know Your Documents**: Accuracy is crucial
✅ **Maintain Relationships**: Good banking relationships help
✅ **Use Multiple Solutions**: Diversify financing
✅ **Keep Records**: Essential for future transactions
✅ **Monitor Changes**: Stay updated on regulations
