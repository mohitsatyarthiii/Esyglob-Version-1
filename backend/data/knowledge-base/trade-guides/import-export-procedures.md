# Import-Export Procedures - Complete Step-by-Step Guide 2026

## Overview

This guide provides detailed step-by-step procedures for importing and exporting goods for B2B businesses.

---

## PART 1: EXPORT PROCEDURES

## Step 1: Market Research

### Research Areas

✅ **Market Size**: Total addressable market
✅ **Competition**: Existing competitors
✅ **Regulations**: Import requirements
✅ **Logistics**: Shipping capabilities
✅ **Cultural Factors**: Business practices

### Research Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Secondary Research** | Online data, reports |
| **Primary Research** | Customer interviews |
| **Trade Data** | Import/export statistics |
| **Trade Shows** | Industry events |
| **Government Data** | Trade promotion agencies |

## Step 2: Product Readiness

### Product Assessment

**Questions to Answer**:
1. What are the product specifications?
2. What certifications are required?
3. What is the product's HS Code?
4. What is the country of origin?
5. What are the packaging requirements?

### Certifications Required

| Product Type | Certifications |
|--------------|----------------|
| **Electronics** | CE, RoHS, FCC |
| **Textiles** | OEKO-TEX, GOTS |
| **Food** | FDA, FSSAI |
| **Chemicals** | REACH, GHS |
| **Steel** | ISO 9001 |

## Step 3: Export Documentation

### Required Documents

**Essential Documents**:

| Document | Purpose | Timing |
|----------|---------|--------|
| **Commercial Invoice** | Transaction details | Before shipment |
| **Packing List** | Package details | Before shipment |
| **Bill of Lading/AWB** | Transport document | At shipment |
| **Certificate of Origin** | Origin proof | Before shipment |
| **Shipping Bill** | Customs declaration | At customs |
| **Insurance Certificate** | Transit insurance | Before shipment |

**Additional Documents**:

| Document | When Required |
|----------|---------------|
| **Export License** | Restricted goods |
| **GSP Certificate** | Preferential tariffs |
| **Health Certificate** | Food/pharma products |
| **Phytosanitary Certificate** | Plant products |
| **Test Report** | Quality certification |

## Step 6: Customs Clearance (Export)

### Export Clearance Process

1. **Document Preparation**
   - Commercial Invoice
   - Packing List
   - Shipping Bill
   - Export Declaration

2. **Customs Filing**
   - Online through ICEGATE (India)
   - Electronic Data Interchange (EDI)

3. **Customs Assessment**
   - Document review
   - Physical inspection (selective)

4. **Let Export Order**
   - Approval from customs
   - Cargo allowed for loading

5. **Loading**
   - Cargo loaded on vessel/aircraft
   - Bill of Lading/AWB issued

---

## Step 7: Shipping and Tracking

### Shipment Process

1. **Booking**: Reserve cargo space
2. **Delivery**: Cargo to port/airport
3. **Loading**: On board vessel/aircraft
4. **Transit**: International movement
5. **Arrival**: At destination port
6. **Customs**: Import clearance

### Tracking Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Carrier Website** | Direct tracking |
| **Forwarder Portal** | Integrated tracking |
| **EsyGlob Platform** | Dashboard tracking |
| **Third-Party Tools** | 17Track, etc. |

## Step 3: Request Samples

### Sample Order Process

1. **Identify Samples**: Select product samples
2. **Request Samples**: Contact supplier
3. **Pay Sample Cost**: Usually supplier charges
4. **Receive and Test**: Verify quality
5. **Evaluate**: Compare to requirements

### Sample Evaluation

**Checklist**:
- [ ] Quality meets specifications
- [ ] Dimensions are correct
- [ ] Color matches (if applicable)
- [ ] Functionality works
- [ ] Packaging is appropriate
- [ ] Documentation is accurate
