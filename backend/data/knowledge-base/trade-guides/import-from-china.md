# Import from China - Complete Guide 2026

## Overview

China remains the world's largest exporter, accounting for over 14% of global exports in 2025. This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to know about importing from China to any destination country.

## Why Import from China?

- **Cost Advantage**: 30-50% lower manufacturing costs compared to Western countries
- **Manufacturing Ecosystem**: Complete supply chain under one roof
- **Scale**: Ability to produce from 100 units to millions
- **Infrastructure**: World-class ports, highways, and logistics network
- **Innovation**: Rapid prototyping and product development

## Step-by-Step Import Process

### 1. Product Research and Sourcing
- **Identify Product**: Define specifications, quality requirements, and target price
- **Supplier Discovery**: Use Alibaba, Global Sources, Made-in-China, or attend Canton Fair
- **Supplier Verification**: Check business licenses, trade records, and certifications
- **Request Samples**: Always order samples before bulk orders

### 2. Negotiation and Contract
- **Price Terms**: FOB (Free On Board), CIF (Cost Insurance Freight), or EXW (Ex Works)
- **Payment Terms**: T/T (30% deposit + 70% before shipment), L/C (Letter of Credit), or D/P (Documents against Payment)
- **Quality Standards**: Define acceptable quality limits (AQL) - typically 1.5-2.5
- **Delivery Timeline**: Production time + shipping time
- **Packaging Requirements**: Export packaging, labeling, branding

### 3. Quality Control
- **Pre-Production Inspection**: Check raw materials
- **During Production Inspection**: Monitor at 30% and 70% completion
- **Pre-Shipment Inspection**: Final quality check before payment balance
- **Third-Party Inspection**: Hire SGS, Bureau Veritas, or TÜV

### 4. Documentation Preparation

**Essential Documents:**
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading (BL) or Air Waybill (AWB)
- Certificate of Origin
- Export License (if applicable)
- Product Certifications (CE, RoHS, FCC, etc.)
- Insurance Certificate

### 5. Shipping Arrangements
- **Sea Freight**: For large volumes (20ft or 40ft containers)
- **Air Freight**: For high-value or urgent shipments
- **Rail Freight**: Via China-Europe Railway Express
- **Courier**: For small packages (DHL, FedEx, UPS)

### 6. Customs Clearance
- **Import Declaration**: Submit to customs in destination country
- **Duty Calculation**: Based on HS Code and product value
- **Payment of Duties**: Customs duties, VAT, and other taxes
- **Customs Inspection**: Random or targeted inspections

### 7. Last Mile Delivery
- **Warehousing**: Store in local warehouse or 3PL
- **Distribution**: Ship to retail locations or direct consumers
- **Returns Management**: Set up returns process for defective items

## Customs Duties and Taxes

### For USA Importers
- **Duty Rates**: 0-25% depending on HS code
- **Section 301 Tariffs**: Additional 7.5-25% on certain Chinese goods
- **Entry Type**: Formal entry (>$2,500) or Informal entry (<$2,500)
- **HTS Classification**: Must use correct Harmonized Tariff Schedule

### For EU Importers
- **Common External Tariff**: 0-15% average
- **VAT**: 17-27% depending on country
- **Anti-dumping Duties**: For specific products like steel, ceramics
- **Origin Rules**: Must prove origin for preferential rates

### For India Importers
- **Basic Customs Duty**: 0-150% (average 15-30%)
- **IGST**: 5-28% (Integrated GST)
- **Surcharge**: 10% on certain goods
- **Anti-dumping**: Duty on steel, chemicals, textiles

## Payment Methods

### Recommended Payment Methods
1. **Letter of Credit (L/C)**: Safest for both parties
2. **T/T with Deposit**: 30% deposit + 70% before shipment
3. **T/T with Balance Against BL**: 30% deposit + 70% against BL copy
4. **DP (Documents against Payment)**: Bank handles document exchange
5. **T/T Balance Against Inspection**: 30% deposit + 70% after inspection

### Avoid
- **100% Advance Payment**: High risk, only for established suppliers
- **100% Before Shipment**: No protection for buyer
- **Western Union**: No recourse for disputes

## Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

### 1. Not Ordering Samples
**Mistake**: Placing bulk order without sample verification.
**Solution**: Always order 5-10 samples and test thoroughly.

### 2. Ignoring Intellectual Property
**Mistake**: Using supplier's designs without checking IP.
**Solution**: Register IP in China and sign NDA/contract.

### 3. Skipping Quality Control
**Mistake**: Assuming QC is supplier's responsibility.
**Solution**: Hire independent third-party inspection.

### 4. Wrong HS Code
**Mistake**: Incorrect classification leading to penalties.
**Solution**: Consult with customs broker and get binding ruling.

### 5. Underestimating Logistics Time
**Mistake**: Not accounting for delays.
**Solution**: Build 2-3 week buffer into schedule.

## Tips for Successful Importing

✅ **Build Relationships**: Long-term relationships lead to better pricing and priority
✅ **Learn Some Chinese**: Even basic Mandarin helps in negotiations
✅ **Visit Supplier Factories**: In-person visits build trust
✅ **Use Trade Insurance**: Protect against non-delivery and non-payment
✅ **Start Small**: Test with small orders before committing to large volumes
✅ **Stay Informed**: Monitor trade policies and tariff changes
✅ **Keep Multiple Suppliers**: Don't rely on a single supplier
✅ **Use a Customs Broker**: Professional brokers minimize customs issues
✅ **Document Everything**: Keep records of all communications and transactions
✅ **Plan for Seasonality**: Chinese New Year and Golden Week affect production

## FAQs

**Q: What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?**
A: Typical MOQ is 100-500 units for electronics, 500-1000 units for apparel, and 1-20 tons for commodities.

**Q: Can I import food products from China?**
A: Yes, but strict regulations apply. Products must be FDA-approved (USA) or have equivalent certifications.

**Q: How do I find reliable suppliers?**
A: Use verified suppliers on Alibaba (Gold Supplier), check trade records, request references, and order samples.

**Q: What are the shipping costs from China?**
A: Sea freight: $2,000-6,000 per 20ft container (depending on destination). Air freight: $5-12/kg.

**Q: Is it safe to pay 30% deposit?**
A: Yes, when using verified suppliers and trade assurance. Ensure balance payment is against inspection or BL.
