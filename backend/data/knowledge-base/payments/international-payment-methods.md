## Detailed Payment Methods

### 1. Cash in Advance

**Description**: Buyer pays before goods are shipped.

**Process**:
1. Buyer sends payment
2. Seller receives payment
3. Seller ships goods
4. Buyer receives goods

**Advantages**:
- Zero risk for seller
- Simple process
- No financing costs

**Disadvantages**:
- High risk for buyer
- No protection for buyer
- Can be expensive

**Best For**:
- First-time transactions
- Small orders
- Trusted sellers
- High-demand products

---

### 2. Letter of Credit (L/C)

**Description**: Bank guarantees payment upon presentation of specified documents.

**Process**:
1. Buyer and seller agree on terms
2. Buyer applies for L/C with issuing bank
3. Issuing bank sends L/C to advising bank
4. Seller reviews and accepts L/C
5. Seller ships goods and presents documents
6. Banks verify documents
7. Payment released to seller

**Types of L/C**:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Irrevocable** | Cannot be changed without all parties |
| **Confirmed** | Another bank guarantees payment |
| **Sight** | Payment immediately on presentation |
| **Usance** | Payment at specific future date |
| **Transferable** | Can be transferred to another supplier |
| **Revolving** | Multiple shipments under same L/C |

**Advantages**:
- Security for both parties
- Bank guarantee
- Clear terms

**Disadvantages**:
- High cost (1-3% of transaction)
- Complex documentation
- Time-consuming

**Best For**:
- Large transactions
- New relationships
- High-value goods
- International trade

---

### 3. Documents against Payment (D/P)

**Description**: Bank releases documents when buyer pays.

**Process**:
1. Seller ships goods
2. Seller presents documents to their bank
3. Documents sent to buyer's bank
4. Buyer pays at their bank
5. Buyer receives documents
6. Buyer takes possession of goods

**Advantages**:
- Security for seller
- Less complex than L/C
- Lower cost than L/C

**Disadvantages**:
- Risk for buyer if goods are rejected
- No guarantee buyer will pay
- Bank only handles documents

**Best For**:
- Established relationships
- Medium-sized transactions

---

### 4. Documents against Acceptance (D/A)

**Description**: Buyer accepts draft and receives documents.

**Process**:
1. Seller ships goods
2. Seller presents documents to bank
3. Documents sent to buyer's bank
4. Buyer accepts draft (promises to pay at future date)
5. Buyer receives documents
6. Buyer takes possession of goods
7. Buyer pays at maturity

**Advantages**:
- Flexible terms
- Less complex than L/C

**Disadvantages**:
- High risk for seller
- No guarantee of payment
- Extended waiting period

**Best For**:
- Long-term relationships
- High-trust transactions

---

### 5. Telegraphic Transfer (T/T)

**Description**: Electronic transfer of funds between banks.

**Process**:
1. Buyer arranges transfer
2. Funds sent through SWIFT network
3. Seller's bank receives funds
4. Funds credited to seller's account

**Types**:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Prepayment** | 100% paid in advance |
| **Deposit + Balance** | 30% deposit, 70% balance |
| **Balance against Documents** | Balance after shipment |

**Advantages**:
- Fast (2-5 business days)
- Lower cost than L/C
- Simple process

**Disadvantages**:
- No protection for either party
- Currency conversion costs
- Banking fees

**Best For**:
- Regular transactions
- Established relationships
- Medium-sized transactions

---

### 6. Open Account

**Description**: Buyer pays after receiving goods.

**Process**:
1. Seller ships goods
2. Buyer receives goods
3. Buyer pays at agreed date (30-120 days)

**Advantages**:
- No upfront payment for buyer
- Simple process
- No banking fees

**Disadvantages**:
- High risk for seller
- No guarantee of payment
- Financing costs for seller

**Best For**:
- Long-term relationships
- High-trust partners
- Repeat customers

---

### 7. EsyGlob Pay (Escrow)

**Description**: Platform holds funds in escrow until delivery confirmed.

**Process**:
1. Buyer pays to EsyGlob escrow
2. Funds held securely
3. Seller ships goods
4. Buyer receives goods
5. Buyer confirms receipt
6. Funds released to seller

**Advantages**:
- Protection for both parties
- Secure processing
- Dispute resolution
- Transparent tracking

**Disadvantages**:
- Slight processing delay
- Transaction fees (1-3%)

**Best For**:
- All transactions
- New relationships
- International trade
