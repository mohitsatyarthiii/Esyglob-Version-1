## Key Trade Agreements

### ASEAN Agreements
- **ASEAN Free Trade Area (AFTA)**: Intra-regional tariff reduction
- **ASEAN-China FTA**: Comprehensive trade agreement
- **ASEAN-Japan CEPA**: Expanded partnership
- **ASEAN-India FTA**: Trade in goods and services
- **ASEAN-Korea FTA**: Comprehensive agreement
- **ASEAN-EU FTA**: Under negotiation

### RCEP (Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership)
**Members**: 15 countries (ASEAN + China, Japan, Korea, etc.)
**Coverage**: 30% of global GDP
**Benefits**: 
- Reduced tariffs
- Uniform rules of origin
- Simplified trade procedures
