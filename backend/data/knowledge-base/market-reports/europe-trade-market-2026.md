## Sustainability and Green Trade

### Green Deal
**Investment**: €1 Trillion
**Goal**: Climate neutrality by 2050
**Key Policies**:
- Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)
- Circular Economy Action Plan
- Sustainable finance

### CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)
**Purpose**: Prevent carbon leakage
**Sectors**: Steel, aluminum, cement, fertilizer, electricity
**Implementation**: 2026 (phased approach)
**Impact**: Carbon cost on imports
