## US Trade Policy Framework

### Key Legislation

#### USMCA (United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement)
- **Replaces**: NAFTA
- **Members**: US, Mexico, Canada
- **Key Benefits**:
  - Auto rules of origin (75% regional content)
  - Digital trade provisions
  - Labor and environmental standards

#### Section 301 Tariffs (China)
| List | Value | Tariff Rate | Status |
|------|-------|-------------|--------|
| List 1 | $50B | 25% | Active |
| List 2 | $50B | 25% | Active |
| List 3 | $200B | 25% | Active |
| List 4A | $120B | 15% | Active |

#### Section 232 Tariffs
- **Steel**: 25%
- **Aluminum**: 10%
- **Countries**: Exemptions for some allies

#### Inflation Reduction Act (IRA)
- **Purpose**: Clean energy investment
- **Investment**: $370 Billion
- **Impact**: Clean tech trade
- **CHIPS Act**: $52 Billion for semiconductor

### US Trade Agreements

## Market Entry Requirements

### Customs and Regulatory

**Key Agencies**
| Agency | Responsibility |
|--------|----------------|
| **CBP** | Customs clearance |
| **FDA** | Food, drugs, cosmetics |
| **FCC** | Electronics |
| **CPSC** | Consumer product safety |
| **USDA** | Agricultural products |
| **EPA** | Environmental regulations |

### Import Requirements

**Required Documents**
1. Commercial Invoice
2. Packing List
3. Bill of Lading/Air Waybill
4. Entry Summary (CBP Form 3461)
5. HS Classification
6. Country of Origin Certificate
7. Product-specific permits

### Customs Process

**Steps**
1. Pre-arrival filing (ISF)
2. Arrival of goods
3. Entry filing by customs broker
4. CBP review and assessment
5. Payment of duties
6. Release of goods
7. Post-entry (audits)

## Tariff Classification

### HTSUS Codes

**Structure**: 10-digit code
- First 6 digits: International HS code
- Last 4 digits: US-specific classification

**Duty Rates**
- General: 0-20% (Most Favored Nation rate)
- Section 301: Additional 15-25% (China)
- Anti-dumping: Product-specific

### How to Classify
1. Identify product's HS code
2. Verify US-specific classification
3. Check for special duties
4. Get binding ruling if needed
5. Maintain records
