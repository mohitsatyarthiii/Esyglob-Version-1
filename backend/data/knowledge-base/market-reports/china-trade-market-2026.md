## Trade Policy Framework

### Key Policies

**"Made in China 2025"**
- **Purpose**: Upgrade manufacturing
- **10 Priority Sectors**: Robotics, EVs, AI, etc.
- **Target**: High-value manufacturing

**Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)**
- **Countries**: 140+ participating countries
- **Investment**: $1 Trillion+
- **Benefits**: Infrastructure, trade routes

**Dual Circulation Strategy**
- **Internal**: Domestic demand
- **External**: International markets
- **Balance**: Self-reliance + openness

**RCEP Participation**
- **Members**: 15 Asia-Pacific countries
- **Benefits**: Tariff reductions
- **Coverage**: 30% of global GDP

## Major Trade Agreements

| Agreement | Partners | Benefits |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| **RCEP** | 14 Asia-Pacific | Tariff reductions, rules of origin |
| **China-ASEAN FTA** | 10 ASEAN | Zero tariffs on 90% products |
| **China-South Korea FTA** | South Korea | Tariff concessions |
| **China-Australia FTA** | Australia | Tariff reductions |
