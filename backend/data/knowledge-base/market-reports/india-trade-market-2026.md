## India's Trade Policy Framework

### Key Export Promotion Schemes

#### 1. RoDTEP (Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products)
- **Purpose**: Refund of embedded taxes
- **Coverage**: All exports
- **Rate**: 0.5-7% of FOB value
- **Benefit**: Cost reduction

#### 2. RoSCTL (Rebate of State and Central Taxes)
- **Purpose**: Refund of state taxes
- **Sectors**: Textiles, apparel
- **Benefit**: Improved competitiveness

#### 3. EPCG (Export Promotion Capital Goods)
- **Purpose**: Duty-free import of machinery
- **Condition**: Export commitment of 6x duty saved
- **Benefit**: Technology upgrade

#### 4. PLI (Production Linked Incentive)
- **Purpose**: Boost manufacturing
- **Sectors**: Electronics, auto, pharma, textiles, etc.
- **Outlay**: ₹1.97 Lakh Crore ($24 Billion)
- **Benefit**: Production incentives
