# India Import-Export Policy - Complete Guide 2026

## Import-Export Code (IEC)

### What is IEC?

IEC is a 10-digit unique identification number required for all import/export activities in India.

### Eligibility
- Any legal entity (Company, LLP, Partnership, Proprietorship)
- Must have valid PAN card
- Must have bank account

### Application Process

**Step 1**: Visit DGFT website (dgft.gov.in)
**Step 2**: Register on the portal
**Step 3**: Complete online application (ANF 2A)
**Step 4**: Upload required documents
**Step 5**: Pay application fee (₹500)
**Step 6**: Receive digital IEC certificate

### Documents Required

| Document | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| **PAN Card** | Mandatory |
| **Bank Certificate/Statement** | Must be from authorized bank |
| **Identity Proof** | Owner/Director ID |
| **Address Proof** | Business address |
| **Digital Signature** | Class 2/3 DSC |

### Validity
- Lifetime validity
- No renewal required
- Must update if details change

## Export Incentives

### RoDTEP (Remission of Duties and Taxes)

**Purpose**: Refund of embedded taxes
**Coverage**: All exports
**Rate**: 0.5-7% of FOB value
**Application**: Online through customs

**Examples**:
| Product Category | Rate |
|------------------|------|
| Textiles | 1-3% |
| Electronics | 2-5% |
| Chemicals | 3-6% |
| Pharma | 2-5% |
| Engineering | 4-7% |

### RoSCTL (Rebate of State and Central Taxes)

**Purpose**: Refund of state taxes
**Sectors**: Textiles and apparel
**Rate**: 0-4% of FOB value
**Benefit**: Improved competitiveness

### EPCG Scheme (Export Promotion Capital Goods)

**Purpose**: Duty-free import of machinery
**Condition**: Export commitment (6x duty saved)
**Validity**: 5 years
**Extension**: Possible

**Calculation Example**:
- Machinery cost: ₹50 Lakhs
- Duty saved: ₹10 Lakhs
- Export commitment: 6 × ₹10 Lakhs = ₹60 Lakhs

### Production Linked Incentive (PLI)

**Sectors**:
| Sector | Outlay (₹ Crore) | Timeline |
|--------|------------------|----------|
| Electronics | 50,000 | 5 years |
| Pharma | 15,000 | 5 years |
| Auto | 25,000 | 5 years |
| Textiles | 10,000 | 5 years |
| Steel | 6,300 | 5 years |

### Duty Drawback

**Purpose**: Refund of customs duties on imported inputs
**Applicable**: Exporters using imported inputs
**Rate**: Fixed or actual

## Customs Procedures

### Import Customs Process

**1. Arrival of Goods**
- Carrier submits IGM
- Goods unloaded at port

**2. Bill of Entry Filing**
- Importer files with customs
- Classification and valuation
- Duty calculation

**3. Customs Assessment**
- Document review
- Valuation verification
- Physical inspection (selective)

**4. Duty Payment**
- Payment of duties
- Various modes available

**5. Clearance**
- Goods released
- Logistics arrangement

### Export Customs Process

**1. Documentation**
- Shipping bill prepared
- Export documents assembled

**2. Customs Declaration**
- Online filing through ICEGATE
- Provide shipping bill details

**3. Customs Assessment**
- Document review
- Physical inspection (selective)

**4. Let Export Order**
- Approval from customs
- Cargo allowed for loading
