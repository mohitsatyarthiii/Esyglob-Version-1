# Global Trade Agreements - Complete Guide 2026

## Overview

Trade agreements are international treaties that reduce trade barriers and facilitate commerce between countries. This guide covers all major trade agreements affecting global B2B trade.

## Types of Trade Agreements

### Classification

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Bilateral** | Between two countries | US-Korea, India-Japan |
| **Regional** | Within a region | USMCA, EU |
| **Multilateral** | Multiple countries | WTO, RCEP |
| **Preferential** | Special preferences | GSP |

### Scope

| Scope | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Goods Only** | Tariff reduction |
| **Goods + Services** | Comprehensive |
| **Comprehensive** | Goods, services, investment |

## Major Trade Agreements

### WTO (World Trade Organization)

**Members**: 164 countries
**Coverage**: Global trade rules
**Key Principles**:
- Most Favored Nation (MFN)
- National Treatment
- Tariff Binding

**Key Agreements**:
| Agreement | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **GATT** | Goods trade |
| **GATS** | Services trade |
| **TRIPS** | Intellectual property |
| **TRIMS** | Investment measures |

### RCEP (Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership)

**Members**: 15 countries
- ASEAN (10)
- China, Japan, South Korea
- Australia, New Zealand

**Coverage**:
- 2.2 Billion people (30% of global)
- 30% of global GDP
- 30% of global trade

**Key Provisions**:
- Tariff reduction (90% over 20 years)
- Rules of origin
- E-commerce provisions
- Intellectual property

### USMCA (United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement)

**Members**: USA, Mexico, Canada
**Population**: 500 Million
**GDP**: $24 Trillion

**Key Provisions**:
- Auto rules of origin (75%)
- Digital trade
- Labor standards
- Environmental provisions

### CPTPP (Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership)

**Members**: 11 countries
- Japan, Australia, Malaysia, Vietnam, etc.

**Features**:
- Progressive trade rules
- High standards
- Investment provisions

### African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA)

**Members**: 54 African nations
**Population**: 1.3 Billion
**GDP**: $3.4 Trillion

**Key Provisions**:
- Single market
- Tariff reduction (90%)
- Services liberalization

## Regional Trade Agreements

### European Union (EU)

**Members**: 27 European countries
**Population**: 450 Million
**GDP**: $18.5 Trillion

**Features**:
- Single market
- Common external tariff
- Free movement of goods

### ASEAN (Association of Southeast Asian Nations)

**Members**: 10 countries
**Population**: 680 Million
**GDP**: $3.8 Trillion

**Features**:
- ASEAN Free Trade Area
- Single market
- Preferential tariffs

### Mercosur

**Members**: Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay (plus associated)
**Population**: 250 Million
**GDP**: $3.3 Trillion

**Features**:
- Common market
- External tariff
- Preferential trade

## Preferential Trade Schemes

### GSP (Generalized System of Preferences)

**Purpose**: Promote developing country exports
**Countries**: 100+ developing countries

| Region | Type | Benefits |
|--------|------|----------|
| **EU GSP** | Standard, GSP+, EBA | Tariff reduction |
| **US GSP** | Standard | Duty-free access |
| **Japan GSP** | Standard | Tariff reduction |

### Special Trade Schemes

| Scheme | Beneficiaries | Benefits |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| **EBA (Everything But Arms)** | Least developed countries | Duty-free access to EU |
| **AGOA (African Growth and Opportunity Act)** | African countries | Duty-free to US |
| **CBI (Caribbean Basin Initiative)** | Caribbean countries | Duty-free to US |

## Rules of Origin

### Purpose
- Prevent trade deflection
- Determine origin for tariffs
- Implement FTAs

### Key Concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Wholly Obtained** | Products entirely from one country |
| **Substantial Transformation** | Significant processing |
| **Value-Added Rule** | Minimum local content (e.g., 35%) |
| **Change in Tariff Classification** | Product classification change |

### FTA-Specific Rules

| Agreement | ROO Rule |
|-----------|----------|
| **RCEP** | 40% Regional Value Content |
| **USMCA** | 75% RVC (auto), 60% others |
| **EU FTAs** | Variable based on product |
| **India FTAs** | 35-50% RVC |

## Compliance Requirements

### Certificate of Origin

**Application Process**:
1. Prepare documents
2. Apply to chamber
3. Submit required docs
4. Pay fee
5. Receive certificate

### Origin Declaration

**Exporter's Responsibility**:
- Accurate information
- Supporting documentation
- Record keeping

### Proof of Origin

**Accepted Documents**:
- Certificate of Origin
- Origin Declaration
- EUR.1 (for EU)
- Invoice Declaration
