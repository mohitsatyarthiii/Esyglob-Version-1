# Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance Guide

## Overview

Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations are critical for preventing financial crimes. This guide helps B2B businesses understand and implement AML compliance.

## What is Money Laundering?

### The Three Stages

| Stage | Description | Examples |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **1. Placement** | Entering illegal funds | Cash deposits, smurfing |
| **2. Layering** | Concealing source | Multiple transfers, shell companies |
| **3. Integration** | Making it legitimate | Investments, real estate |

### Common Methods

- Shell companies
- Trade-based laundering
- Cryptocurrency
- Real estate
- Structured transactions

## KYC/KYB (Know Your Customer/Business)

### Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

**Standard CDD**:
- ✅ Customer identification
- ✅ Identity verification
- ✅ Beneficial ownership
- ✅ Risk assessment

**Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)**:
- ✅ Additional verification
- ✅ Source of funds
- ✅ Source of wealth
- ✅ Enhanced monitoring

### Sanctions Screening

**Check Against**:
- OFAC list
- EU sanctions
- UN sanctions
- Local sanctions

### PEP Screening

**What**: Politically Exposed Persons
**Why**: Higher risk of corruption
**Requires**: Enhanced due diligence

## Transaction Monitoring

### Red Flags

| Red Flag | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Unusual Amounts** | Round numbers, large changes |
| **Unusual Frequency** | Many small transactions |
| **Unusual Pattern** | Unusual routing |
| **Unusual Customers** | High-risk locations |
| **Unusual Products** | Inconsistent with business |

### Suspicious Activity Indicators

| Indicator | Examples |
|-----------|----------|
| **Structuration** | Breaking amounts below threshold |
| **Third-party involvement** | Payments from unrelated parties |
| **Inconsistency** | Business not matching activities |
| **Complexity** | Unnecessarily complex structures |

---

## Reporting Obligations

### Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR)

**When to File**:
- Suspected money laundering
- Unusual/suspicious transactions
- No minimum threshold

**Content**:
- Customer information
- Transaction details
- Suspicion description
- Supporting documentation

### Timeline

| Report Type | Deadline |
|-------------|----------|
| **SAR** | Within 30 days |
| **Urgent SAR** | Within 24 hours |
