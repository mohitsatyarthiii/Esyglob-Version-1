# EsyGlob Buyer Guide - Complete Walkthrough

## Introduction

Welcome to EsyGlob! This comprehensive guide will help you navigate the platform as a buyer and make successful international purchases.

## Account Setup

### Step 1: Registration

**How to Register**:
1. Go to esyglob.com
2. Click "Sign Up" button
3. Enter your business email
4. Create a strong password
5. Select "Buyer" role
6. Agree to Terms & Conditions
7. Click "Create Account"

### Step 2: Email Verification

1. Check your email inbox
2. Find verification email
3. Click verification link
4. Account activated

### Step 3: Complete Profile

**Required Information**:
- Company name
- Business registration number
- Tax ID/GST number
- Business address
- Contact person details
- Phone number

**Optional Information**:
- Company logo
- Business website
- Product interests
- Preferred currencies

### Step 4: Business Verification

**Required Documents**:
| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Business Registration** | Verify legal entity |
| **Tax ID/GST** | Tax compliance |
| **ID Proof** | Owner/Director identity |
| **Bank Details** | Payment processing |
| **Trade License** | Import license (if required) |

**Verification Timeline**:
| Type | Timeframe |
|------|-----------|
| Basic | 24-48 hours |
| Standard | 3-5 days |
| Premium | 5-10 days |

## Placing Orders

### Step 1: Request Quote

**For Custom Orders**:
1. Go to product page
2. Click "Request Quote"
3. Enter required details:
   - Quantity needed
   - Product specifications
   - Delivery requirements
   - Any special instructions
4. Submit request

### Step 2: Negotiation

**Negotiation Tips**:
- Research market prices
- Consider total landed cost
- Negotiate MOQ flexibility
- Discuss payment terms
- Request samples
- Discuss shipping arrangements
- Document all communication

### Step 3: Order Processing

**Order Workflow**:
1. **Order Placed**: Buyer confirms order
2. **Payment**: Buyer pays through EsyGlob Pay
3. **Confirmation**: Seller confirms order
4. **Production**: Seller produces goods
5. **Quality Check**: Quality inspection
6. **Shipping**: Goods shipped
7. **Delivery**: Goods received
8. **Confirmation**: Buyer confirms receipt

## Payment Methods

### EsyGlob Pay (Recommended)

**How It Works**:
1. Buyer pays to EsyGlob escrow
2. Funds secured in trust account
3. Seller ships goods
4. Buyer confirms receipt
5. Funds released to seller

**Benefits**:
- Buyer protection
- Seller protection
- Secure transactions
- Dispute resolution

**Step 1**: Document Issue
- Take photos/videos
- Keep all evidence
- Record communication

**Step 2**: Contact Seller
- Reach out directly
- Try to resolve
- Document all communication

**Step 3**: File Dispute
- Go to Claims Center
- Provide evidence
- Submit claim

**Step 4**: Investigation
- Platform reviews
- Both parties contacted
- Decision made

**Step 5**: Resolution
- Refund processed
- Replacement arranged
- Compensation provided

## Tips for Successful Buying

### Best Practices

✅ **Verify Suppliers**: Always check verification status
✅ **Order Samples**: Test before bulk orders
✅ **Use EsyGlob Pay**: Secure payment protection
✅ **Document Everything**: Keep all records
✅ **Communicate Clearly**: Be specific in requirements
✅ **Check Total Cost**: Consider all costs (duties, freight)
✅ **Build Relationships**: Long-term partnerships work best
✅ **Read Reviews**: Learn from other buyers
✅ **Use Platform Tools**: Duty calculator, currency converter

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Skipping Verification**: Not checking supplier status
❌ **No Sample Order**: Ordering bulk without testing
❌ **Payment Outside Platform**: Loss of protection
❌ **No Documentation**: Not keeping records
❌ **Ignoring Customs**: Not understanding import duties
❌ **Poor Communication**: Not being clear on requirements
❌ **Rushing Decisions**: Not doing due diligence

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I find the best price?**
A: Compare multiple suppliers, request quotes, negotiate, and consider total landed cost.

**Q: What if I don't like the product?**
A: Check seller's return policy. For quality issues, file a claim under Buyer Protection.

**Q: Can I change my order after placing it?**
A: Contact seller immediately. Changes are easier before production starts.

**Q: What currency should I transact in?**
A: USD is recommended. Use EsyGlob's currency converter for estimates.

**Q: How do I get trade finance?**
A: EsyGlob offers buyer financing. Contact our trade finance team.

**Q: What if the supplier is unresponsive?**
A: Contact EsyGlob support. We'll help mediate or find alternative suppliers.

**Q: How long does shipping take?**
A: 15-45 days by sea, 3-7 days by air, 2-5 days by express courier.

**Q: What are customs duties?**
A: Taxes imposed by your country on imports. Use our duty calculator for estimates.
