# EsyGlob Buyer Protection Program

## Overview

EsyGlob Buyer Protection is a comprehensive program designed to protect B2B buyers during international transactions. This guide explains all aspects of the program for secure and confident trading.

## What is EsyGlob Buyer Protection?

EsyGlob Buyer Protection is a guarantee program that protects eligible B2B buyers when purchasing through the EsyGlob platform. The program covers:

- **Non-delivery**: Goods not received
- **Quality Issues**: Products not matching description or sample
- **Payment Protection**: Secure payment processing
- **Dispute Resolution**: Mediation and arbitration services

## Coverage Details

### What's Covered?

| Coverage Type | Protection Period | Maximum Amount |
|---------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Non-Delivery** | 60 days from payment | Up to $100,000 |
| **Quality Discrepancy** | 14 days after delivery | Up to $100,000 |
| **Counterfeit Products** | 30 days after delivery | Up to $100,000 |
| **Shipping Damage** | 7 days after delivery | Up to $100,000 |
| **Incorrect Quantity** | 14 days after delivery | Up to $100,000 |

### What's NOT Covered?
- **Natural Disasters**: Acts of God, force majeure
- **Buyer Misuse**: Not following proper procedures
- **Unverified Suppliers**: Non-verified or unrated suppliers
- **Third-party Damage**: Damage occurring outside EsyGlob ecosystem
- **Customs Delays**: Customs-related issues
- **Loss of Opportunity**: Consequential damages

## Eligibility Requirements

### Buyer Requirements
- **Verified Account**: Complete verification process
- **Use EsyGlob Pay**: Payment must use platform's payment system
- **Product Listed**: Product must be listed on EsyGlob
- **Trackable Shipment**: Must have tracking information
- **Documentation**: Keep all transaction records

### Transaction Requirements
- **Transaction Amount**: Minimum $500, maximum $100,000
- **Product Category**: Eligible categories
- **Shipping Terms**: Must use shipping method with tracking
- **Inspection**: Within 48 hours of delivery (quality claim)
- **Timely Reporting**: Must report issues promptly

## Claim Process

### Step 1: Issue Discovery
- Identify and document the issue
- Take clear photos and videos
- Collect all evidence

### Step 2: Communication
- Contact supplier directly first
- Attempt to resolve amicably
- Use platform communication
- Document all communication

### Step 3: File Claim
- Access EsyGlob Claims Center
- Provide all documentation
- Describe the issue in detail
- Upload supporting evidence

### Step 4: Investigation
- Platform reviews documentation
- May request additional information
- Contact both parties
- Investigation takes 7-14 days

### Step 5: Resolution
- **Supplier Resolves**: Issue fixed
- **Partial Refund**: Buyer receives partial amount
- **Full Refund**: Buyer receives full amount
- **Escrow Release**: Determine who gets payment
- **Mediation**: Third-party mediation if needed

## Documentation Requirements

### For Non-Delivery Claims
- Payment confirmation
- Order confirmation
- Shipping details (tracking number)
- Proof of delivery attempt (if any)
- Communication records with supplier
- Screenshots of tracking

### For Quality Issues
- Photos and videos of the issue
- Quality inspection report (if available)
- Sample comparison photos (if applicable)
- Product specifications from listing
- Professional assessment (if needed)

### For Counterfeit Claims
- Authentication test results
- Comparison with genuine product
- Certificate of authenticity (if available)
- Expert opinion (if available)

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Order Not Received
- Buyer files non-delivery claim
- Supplier provides tracking info
- Platform investigates
- If supplier can't prove delivery: Buyer gets refund
- If proof of delivery: Claim denied

### Scenario 2: Quality Discrepancy
- Buyer files quality claim
- Compare product with description
- Photos and evidence evaluated
- If product doesn't match description: Supplier corrects or refund
- If product is acceptable: Claim denied

### Scenario 3: Incorrect Quantity
- Buyer files quantity claim
- Check delivery vs. order
- Provide packing list evidence
- If shortage confirmed: Supplier ships missing items or refunds
- If quantity correct: Claim denied
