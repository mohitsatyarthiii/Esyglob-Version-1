# EsyGlob Payment System - Complete Guide

## Overview

EsyGlob provides a secure, reliable, and efficient payment system for international B2B transactions. This guide covers all payment-related features and processes.

## EsyGlob Pay - Escrow Protection

### How It Works

**Escrow Process**:

| Step | Description | Timeline |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **1. Payment** | Buyer pays to EsyGlob escrow | Day 1 |
| **2. Hold** | Funds held in trust account | Until delivery |
| **3. Fulfillment** | Seller ships goods | Varies |
| **4. Confirmation** | Buyer confirms receipt | Delivery |
| **5. Release** | Funds released to seller | Within 2-5 days |

**Escrow Benefits**:

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Buyer Protection** | Funds secured until delivery |
| **Seller Protection** | Payment guaranteed |
| **Secure Processing** | PCI-DSS compliant |
| **Dispute Resolution** | Mediation available |
| **Transparency** | Full tracking |

### Payment Flow

**Visual Process**:


Buyer → Pays to Escrow (EsyGlob)
↓
Escrow Holds Funds
↓
Seller Ships Goods
↓
Buyer Receives Goods
↓
Buyer Confirms Receipt
↓
Escrow Releases Funds to Seller
↓
Transaction Complete

## Dispute Resolution

### Dispute Process

**Step 1**: Communication
- Buyer contacts seller directly
- Attempt to resolve amicably

**Step 2**: Platform Mediation
- Escalate to EsyGlob
- Both parties provide evidence
- Platform facilitates resolution

**Step 3**: Payment Decision
| Decision | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **Release to Seller** | Payment released |
| **Refund to Buyer** | Payment returned |
| **Partial Release** | Split payment |
| **Further Investigation** | Extended review |

### Dispute Timeline

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| **Communication** | 3-7 days |
| **Mediation** | 7-14 days |
