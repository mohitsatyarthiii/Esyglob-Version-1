# Buyers FAQ - Everything You Need to Know

## Account and Registration

### Q1: How do I create a buyer account on EsyGlob?
**A:** Visit EsyGlob.com, click "Sign Up", choose "Buyer" as your role, fill in your business details, verify your email and phone number, and complete your profile. Account creation takes 5-10 minutes.

### Q2: What documents do I need to register as a buyer?
**A:** You need: Business registration certificate, GST/PAN/Tax ID, Bank account details, and Business email. Some products require additional compliance documents based on your country and product category.

### Q3: Is it free to create a buyer account?
**A:** Yes, creating a buyer account is free. You only pay for products you purchase and any applicable transaction fees. No monthly membership fees for buyers.

### Q4: Can I have multiple buyer accounts?
**A:** No. Each business entity can have only one buyer account. Multiple accounts for the same business are prohibited and will be flagged for review.

## Product Search and Discovery

### Q5: How do I find the right product on EsyGlob?
**A:** Use the search bar to enter product name, category, or HS code. Apply filters like price range, MOQ, verification level, and country of origin. You can also browse categories and use AI-powered product recommendations.

### Q6: What is an HS Code and why is it important?
**A:** HS Code (Harmonized System Code) is an international product classification system. It's essential for customs clearance and determining import duties. You'll need the correct HS code for your product when ordering.

### Q7: Can I order samples before bulk purchase?
**A:** Yes. Most sellers offer samples. Order samples to verify quality, specifications, and functionality before committing to bulk orders. Sample costs vary by product.

### Q8: How do I know if a seller is reliable?
**A:** Check the seller's verification badge (Blue = Verified, Gold = Premium Verified), read buyer reviews and ratings, review their transaction history, contact their previous buyers, and check their business registration details.

## Orders and Payments

### Q9: How do I place an order on EsyGlob?
**A:** Select your product, choose quantity and specifications, click "Add to Cart", review your order, choose payment method, complete payment through EsyGlob Pay, and track order status in your dashboard.

### Q10: What payment methods are accepted?
**A:** We accept: Bank Transfer (Wire Transfer), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), UPI (India), PayPal, EsyGlob Wallet, and Letter of Credit (for large transactions).

### Q11: Is my payment secure on EsyGlob?
**A:** Yes. All payments are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. Payments are held in escrow and only released to the seller after you confirm receipt of goods.

### Q12: What currency are prices listed in?
**A:** Prices are displayed in USD by default. You can view prices in your preferred currency (EUR, GBP, INR, JPY) using our currency converter. The final transaction is processed in the seller's preferred currency.

### Q13: Can I negotiate prices with sellers?
**A:** Yes. EsyGlob supports price negotiation. Use the "Inquiry" or "Request Quote" feature to start negotiations. You can also negotiate MOQ, payment terms, and shipping terms.

### Q14: What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
**A:** MOQ varies by seller and product. Check the product listing for MOQ details. For bulk items, MOQ may be high. You can negotiate lower MOQ with sellers for trial orders.

### Q15: How long does it take to process my order?
**A:** Processing time varies: Sample orders: 3-7 days, Bulk orders: 7-30 days production time + shipping time. Check each product listing for estimated processing time.

## Shipping and Logistics

### Q16: How long does shipping take?
**A:** Shipping time depends on destination and method: Air freight: 3-7 days, Sea freight: 15-45 days, Express courier: 2-5 days. Check shipping options during checkout.

### Q17: How do I track my shipment?
**A:** Once shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email. Track your shipment through: EsyGlob dashboard, Carrier website (DHL, FedEx, UPS), or the seller's provided tracking portal.

### Q18: What are the shipping costs?
**A:** Shipping costs are calculated based on: Weight and dimensions, Shipping method, Destination country, Insurance coverage, and Customs clearance. Costs are shown during checkout.

### Q19: Who handles customs clearance?
**A:** Customs clearance responsibility depends on the Incoterm: FOB: Buyer handles import clearance, CIF: Seller handles to destination, DDP: Seller handles all clearance. Check your order's Incoterm.

### Q20: What documents do I need for customs clearance?
**A:** Required documents: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading/Air Waybill, Certificate of Origin, Import License (if required), Insurance Certificate, and any product-specific certifications.

### Q21: What if my shipment is damaged or lost?
**A:** Contact the seller immediately and file a claim. For insured shipments, the insurance company will handle. EsyGlob Buyer Protection also covers shipping damage if you purchased through the platform.

## Quality and Returns

### Q22: What if the product quality doesn't match expectations?
**A:** Contact the seller within 48 hours of delivery. Provide photos/videos of the issue. If you can't resolve, file a dispute through EsyGlob's dispute resolution system. Quality assurance is covered under our Buyer Protection.

### Q23: What is EsyGlob Buyer Protection?
**A:** EsyGlob Buyer Protection covers: Non-delivery (100% refund), Quality discrepancy (up to 100% refund), Counterfeit products (100% refund), Shipping damage (up to 100% refund), and Incorrect quantity (adjustment or refund). Terms apply.

### Q24: Can I return products I don't like?
**A:** Returns depend on the seller's policy and your agreement. Some sellers accept returns for quality issues only. Check seller's return policy before ordering. Customized products generally cannot be returned.

### Q25: What is the dispute resolution process?
**A:** 1. Contact seller directly (try to resolve), 2. File dispute on EsyGlob (within 60 days), 3. Provide all evidence, 4. Platform investigates (7-14 days), 5. Resolution: Refund, replacement, or compensation, 6. Appeal if unsatisfied.

## Certification and Compliance

### Q26: What certifications should I look for?
**A:** Look for: ISO 9001 (Quality Management), ISO 14001 (Environmental Management), CE (European market), UL (US electrical safety), RoHS (Electronics), GMP (Pharmaceuticals), and product-specific certifications.

### Q27: How do I verify product certifications?
**A:** Ask the seller for certificate copies, verify with the issuing authority, check for validity dates, use third-party verification services, and check if certifications are recognized in your country.

### Q28: What are the import regulations in my country?
**A:** Import regulations vary by country. Check: Your country's customs website, Trade associations, Customs brokers, or Contact our trade consultants for assistance.

### Q29: Are there any prohibited products on EsyGlob?
**A:** Yes. Prohibited products include: Narcotics and drugs, Weapons and ammunition, Counterfeit goods, Hazardous materials, Stolen goods, and Endangered species products.

## Payments and Finance

### Q30: What are the transaction fees?
**A:** Transaction fees: Buyer pays 0%, Seller pays 1-3% transaction fee, Payment gateway fee (1.5-2%) absorbed by seller, and Foreign exchange fees (if applicable).

### Q31: Can I get trade finance through EsyGlob?
**A:** Yes. We offer: Buyer financing (for purchases), Seller financing (for sellers), Supply chain finance (optimize working capital), Trade credit insurance (protect against defaults), and Letter of Credit services.

### Q32: How do I get a refund?
**A:** Refund process: 1. File a claim/dispute, 2. Platform investigates, 3. If approved, refund is processed, 4. Refund to original payment method, 5. Processing time: 5-10 business days.

## Account Management

### Q33: How do I change my account details?
**A:** Login to your account, go to "My Account" > "Settings", update your business details, contact information, or preferences, verify changes (if required), and save.

### Q34: How do I close my buyer account?
**A:** Login to your account, go to "My Account" > "Settings" > "Close Account", confirm all pending transactions are complete, download your data (if needed), and click "Close Account".

### Q35: What happens to my data if I close my account?
**A:** Your data is retained for: 7 years (tax and legal compliance), 3 years (transaction records), and Some data is anonymized for analytics. You can request data deletion after the mandatory retention period.
