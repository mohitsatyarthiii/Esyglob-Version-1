# Payments and Shipping FAQ

## Payments

### Q1: What payment methods are accepted on EsyGlob?
**A:** Accepted payment methods: Bank Transfer/Wire Transfer, Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), UPI (India), PayPal, EsyGlob Wallet, and Letter of Credit (for large transactions).

### Q2: Is my payment information secure?
**A:** Yes. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. Your payment information is encrypted and not stored on our servers. We use 256-bit AES encryption for all transactions.

### Q3: What is EsyGlob Pay?
**A:** EsyGlob Pay is our secure escrow payment system. The buyer pays into this system, and the funds are held in a third-party trust account. The payment is released to the seller only after the buyer confirms satisfactory delivery.

### Q4: How does the escrow payment work?
**A:** 1. Buyer sends payment to EsyGlob escrow account, 2. Seller is notified, 3. Seller ships product, 4. Buyer receives and inspects, 5. Buyer confirms satisfaction, 6. Funds released to seller, 7. Transaction complete.

### Q5: How long does it take for payments to settle?
**A:** Payment settlement timeline: Payment receipt (immediately), Payment verification (24 hours), Escrow holding (until buyer confirms), Seller payout (2-5 business days after release), Total: 7-30 days depending on shipping.

### Q6: Can I make partial payments?
**A:** Yes. Common arrangements: 30% deposit + 70% before shipment, 50% deposit + 50% after delivery, and Multiple installments (negotiated with seller).

### Q7: How do I get a refund?
**A:** 1. Initiate a dispute through platform, 2. Provide evidence, 3. Platform investigates, 4. If approved, refund is processed, 5. Refund to original payment method, 6. 5-10 business days for processing.

### Q8: What are the platform transaction fees?
**A:** Buyer fees: 0% (no buyer transaction fees). Seller fees: 1-3% transaction fee, payment gateway fees (1.5-2%), and withdrawal fees ($1-5). Fees are displayed during the transaction process.

### Q9: What currency are transactions processed in?
**A:** Transactions can be processed in: USD (recommended), EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, and AUD. Choose your preferred currency during checkout. Currency conversion fees may apply.

### Q10: How do I convert currencies?
**A:** Currency conversion: Real-time exchange rates, All major currencies supported, Conversion fees apply (0.5-2%), Displayed rate at checkout, and Approximate final amount shown.

## Shipping and Logistics

### Q11: What are the shipping options?
**A:** Available shipping options: Sea freight (best for bulk), Air freight (best for urgent), Express courier (best for samples), Rail freight (Eurasia route), and Truck freight (regional).

### Q12: How long does shipping take?
**A:** Typical shipping times: Sea freight: 15-45 days, Air freight: 3-7 days, Express courier: 2-5 days, Rail freight: 18-25 days (China to Europe), and Truck freight: 5-15 days (regional).

### Q13: How much does shipping cost?
**A:** Shipping costs depend on: Weight and dimensions, Shipping method, Destination country, Insurance level, and Handling fees. Costs are shown at checkout. Get shipping quotes before ordering.

### Q14: How do I calculate shipping costs?
**A:** Use our shipping calculator: Enter product weight and dimensions, Select shipping method, Choose destination country, Add insurance value, Include handling fees, and Get estimated cost.

### Q15: Who handles customs clearance?
**A:** Responsibility depends on Incoterms: FOB: Buyer handles import clearance, CIF: Seller handles to destination, DDP: Seller handles all clearance, and EXW: Buyer handles all logistics.

### Q16: What documents are required for shipping?
**A:** Required documents: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading/Air Waybill, Certificate of Origin, Insurance Certificate, Export/Import License, and Product-specific permits.

### Q17: How do I track my shipment?
**A:** Track through: EsyGlob platform dashboard, Carrier website (DHL, FedEx, UPS, Maersk), Email updates from carrier, and SMS notifications.

### Q18: What happens if my shipment is delayed?
**A:** If delayed: Check tracking for reason, Contact carrier directly, Contact seller for assistance, Escalate to platform if significant delay, and Consider claiming insurance if allowed.

### Q19: What if my shipment is damaged?
**A:** 1. Document damage with photos, 2. Notify seller immediately, 3. File insurance claim (if applicable), 4. Keep damaged packaging, 5. File platform dispute if necessary, and 6. Follow carrier's claims process.

### Q20: Can I ship Dangerous Goods/Hazmat?
**A:** Dangerous goods require: Special packaging, Proper labeling, Documentation, Trained personnel, Carrier approval, and Regulatory compliance. Check with carrier before shipping.

## Insurance

### Q21: Is shipping insurance available?
**A:** Yes. Insurance options: Standard (included with CIF/CIP), Enhanced (additional coverage), All-risk coverage, and Specific risk coverage. Insurance protects against loss or damage during transit.

### Q22: How much does shipping insurance cost?
**A:** Insurance costs: Typically 0.5-2% of goods value, Minimum premium applies, Higher for high-risk goods, and Volume discounts available.

### Q23: How do I claim shipping insurance?
**A:** 1. Document damage/incident, 2. Notify seller and carrier, 3. Obtain inspection report, 4. Submit claim with evidence, 5. Insurance provider investigates, 6. Claim settled within 30-60 days.

## Customs and Duties

### Q24: What are customs duties?
**A:** Customs duties are taxes imposed on imported goods. They include: Import duties (percentage of goods value), VAT/GST (value-added tax), Excise duties (specific products), and Anti-dumping duties (for unfair trade).

### Q25: Who pays customs duties?
**A:** Responsibility depends on Incoterms: DDP: Seller pays all duties, DAP: Buyer pays import duties, FOB/CIF: Buyer pays import duties, EXW: Buyer pays all duties.

### Q26: How do I find out my country's customs duties?
**A:** Check: Your country's customs website (e.g., CBP.gov for US), Customs broker, Trade associations, Duty calculator tools, and EsyGlob's duty calculator.

### Q27: What is the HS Code?
**A:** HS Code (Harmonized System Code) is an international product classification. It determines: Duty rates, Import restrictions, and Statistics tracking. Use correct HS Code for accurate duties.

### Q28: What happens if I misclassify my product?
**A:** Consequences include: Paying incorrect duties, Customs delays, Fines and penalties, Product seizure, and Legal action. Always classify correctly.

### Q29: Can I get a customs duty refund?
**A:** Duty refunds possible through: Overpayment claims, Re-export (if not needed), Temporary imports, and Duty drawback programs. Follow your country's procedures.

## Storage and Warehousing

### Q30: What are the warehousing options?
**A:** Options include: Seller's warehouse, Third-party logistics (3PL), Port warehouses, Bonded warehouses, and Fulfillment centers.

### Q31: What is the storage cost?
**A:** Costs vary: Per pallet/per shelf, Per square foot/per month, Handling fees, Insurance, and Value-added services.

### Q32: How do I choose a warehouse?
**A:** Consider: Location (near ports/transport), Security, Facilities, Cost, Service quality, Insurance coverage, and Regulatory compliance.

## Returns and Refunds

### Q33: What is the return policy?
**A:** Policy depends on: Seller's policy, Product type, Reason for return, Time since delivery, and Condition of goods. Check seller's return policy before ordering.

### Q34: Can I return products?
**A:** Return accepted for: Quality issues, Damaged goods, Incorrect items, and Product not as described. Customized products generally cannot be returned.

### Q35: How do I return products?
**A:** 1. Contact seller within 7 days, 2. Get return authorization, 3. Package properly, 4. Ship to seller's address, 5. Provide tracking, 6. Seller inspects, 7. Refund processed.

### Q36: Who pays return shipping?
**A:** Responsibility: Seller pays if product is faulty, Buyer pays if change of mind, Split if mutual agreement, and Depends on terms.

## Packaging

### Q37: What are the packaging requirements?
**A:** Requirements: Export-worthy packaging, Proper labeling, Protection against damage, Appropriate for shipping method, Compliance with regulations, and Moisture protection for sea freight.

### Q38: What is export packaging?
**A:** Export packaging includes: Carton boxes, Pallets, Shrink wrap, Waterproofing, Cushioning materials, and Proper labeling (handling instructions, country of origin).

### Q39: How do I label packages?
**A:** Labeling requirements: Country of origin, Product name/description, Consignee address, Consignor address, Handling instructions (Fragile, This Side Up), Weight and dimensions, and Tracking numbers.

## Tips and Best Practices

### Q40: What are the best practices for shipping?
**A:** 1. Choose appropriate shipping method, 2. Proper packaging, 3. Accurate documentation, 4. Insurance coverage, 5. Tracking and monitoring, 6. Clear communication with buyer, 7. Factor in transit time, and 8. Plan for contingencies.

### Q41: How can I save on shipping costs?
**A:** Savings strategies: Consolidate orders, Choose sea freight for bulk, Negotiate carrier rates, Use online shipping platforms, Optimize packaging, and Plan ahead.

### Q42: What should I do if customs holds my shipment?
**A:** 1. Check what documents are needed, 2. Provide additional information, 3. Contact customs broker, 4. Pay any duties, 5. Follow customs procedures, 6. Check tracking for updates, 7. Contact seller/platform if needed.

### Q43: How do I choose a reliable shipping partner?
**A:** Evaluate: Reputation and reliability, Network coverage, Cost competitiveness, Service quality, Tracking capabilities, Insurance options, and Customs expertise.

### Q44: What is DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)?
**A:** DDP means: Seller is responsible for, All shipping costs, Insurance, Import duties and taxes, Customs clearance, and Delivery to buyer's address. It's most convenient for buyers.

### Q45: What is FOB (Free On Board)?
**A:** FOB means: Seller responsible until goods on board vessel, Seller pays: export clearance, local transport, and loading, Buyer pays: freight, insurance, and import clearance, and Risk transfers when goods on board.

## Tracking and Monitoring

### Q46: How do I track international shipments?
**A:** Tracking methods: Shipping line/Airline website, Freight forwarder portal, EsyGlob platform, Third-party tracking tools (17Track), and Email notifications.

### Q47: What is the difference between a Bill of Lading and Air Waybill?
**A:** Bill of Lading: For sea freight, Is a title document and transferable, and Used for letter of credit. Air Waybill: For air freight, Is a contract of carriage, and Not a title document.

### Q48: How do I get a Bill of Lading?
**A:** 1. Book shipment with shipping line, 2. Provide shipping instructions, 3. Submit export documentation, 4. Pay freight charges, 5. Receive draft BL, 6. Verify details, and 7. Release original BL.

## Emergency Situations

### Q49: What if my shipment is lost?
**A:** 1. Check tracking thoroughly, 2. File missing shipment report with carrier, 3. Contact seller/shipper, 4. File insurance claim, 5. File platform dispute, and 6. Consider alternative arrangements.

### Q50: What if the seller doesn't ship?
**A:** 1. Contact seller immediately, 2. Provide order details, 3. Check order cancellation, 4. If no response, file platform dispute, 5. Request refund, and 6. Consider alternative supplier.

### Q51: What if I suspect fraud?
**A:** 1. Stop all communication, 2. Don't make payments outside platform, 3. Report to platform immediately, 4. Provide all evidence, 5. Block the seller, and 6. Contact authorities if needed.
