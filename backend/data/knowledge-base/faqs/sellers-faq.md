# Sellers FAQ - Complete Guide for Exporters

## Account Registration

### Q1: How do I register as a seller on EsyGlob?
**A:** Visit EsyGlob.com, click "Sign Up", select "Seller" as role, choose your business type (Exporter, Manufacturer, Trader), provide business details, verify your email and phone, submit required documents for verification.

### Q2: What documents do I need for seller verification?
**A:** Required documents: Business registration certificate, GST/PAN/Tax ID, Import Export Code (IEC) for exporters, Bank account details, Company profile, and Owner/Director ID proof.

### Q3: How long does verification take?
**A:** Basic verification: 24-48 hours, Standard verification: 3-5 business days, Premium verification: 5-10 business days. Expedited processing available for Premium sellers.

### Q4: What is the difference between verification levels?
**A:** **Basic**: Email & phone verification, limited listings, low transaction limits. **Standard**: Full business verification, more listings, higher limits. **Premium**: Factory audit, ISO certification, unlimited listings & limits, featured status.

### Q5: Are there any fees for seller registration?
**A:** Registration is free. Verification fees: Basic (Free), Standard (₹1,000/$12 annual), Premium (₹5,000/$60 annual). Transaction fees: 1-3% on successful sales.

## Product Listings

### Q6: How do I list my products on EsyGlob?
**A:** Go to "My Account" > "Sell Products" > "Add New Product", upload product images and videos, fill in product details (name, description, specifications), set pricing and MOQ, select product category and HS code, list certifications, and publish.

### Q7: What information should I include in product listings?
**A:** Include: Clear product title and description, High-quality images (multiple angles), Detailed specifications, Price and MOQ, Certifications, Delivery timeline, Payment terms, Shipping options, and Return policy.

### Q8: How many products can I list?
**A:** Basic: Up to 50 products, Standard: Up to 200 products, Premium: Unlimited listings.

### Q9: What are the image requirements?
**A:** Images: Minimum 800x800 pixels, JPEG/PNG format, Multiple angles required, Product photos only (no watermarks), Professional lighting, and Show product in use if relevant.

### Q10: How do I set competitive pricing?
**A:** Research competitor pricing, factor in all costs (production, shipping, platform fees, profit margin), consider volume discounts, set currency in USD/EUR/GBP, and review and update pricing regularly.

## Orders and Fulfillment

### Q11: How do I receive orders on EsyGlob?
**A:** Orders are received through your seller dashboard. You'll get: Email notification, SMS alert, Dashboard notification, and Order details including buyer information and shipping address.

### Q12: What is the order fulfillment process?
**A:** 1. Acknowledge order within 24 hours, 2. Confirm stock availability, 3. Prepare and pack goods, 4. Arrange shipping, 5. Upload tracking information, 6. Upload required documents, 7. Mark order as "Shipped", 8. Track until delivery.

### Q13: How long do I have to ship orders?
**A:** Standard: 7-14 business days, Customized products: 15-30 business days, Expedited: 3-5 business days (if offered). The timeframe is displayed to buyers at checkout.

### Q14: What shipping methods are available?
**A:** Sea freight (large orders, 15-45 days), Air freight (urgent, 3-7 days), Express courier (small orders, 2-5 days), Rail freight (Eurasia route), and Truck freight (regional shipping).

### Q15: How do I handle order cancellations?
**A:** If buyer requests cancellation: Before production: 100% refund, During production: Partial refund (deduct production costs), After shipping: No refund (insurance claim instead), and Always communicate with buyer throughout.

## Payments and Settlements

### Q16: How do I receive payments from buyers?
**A:** Payments are processed through: EsyGlob Pay (escrow service), Direct Bank Transfer, Letter of Credit, and PayPal. Payment is released after you've delivered and buyer confirms receipt.

### Q17: What is the payout schedule?
**A:** Standard: 14 days after delivery confirmation, Premium sellers: 7 days after delivery, Express sellers: 24 hours for small transactions. Payouts are processed on Tuesdays and Fridays.

### Q18: What currencies can I receive payments in?
**A:** Available currencies: USD (recommended), EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, and AUD. You can choose your preferred payout currency.

### Q19: What are the seller fees on EsyGlob?
**A:** Listing fee: Free, Transaction fee: 1-3% of order value, Payment gateway fee: 1.5-2%, Withdrawal fee: $1-5 per withdrawal, Membership fee: $0-99/month (optional).

### Q20: How do I withdraw my earnings?
**A:** 1. Go to "My Account" > "Wallet", 2. Click "Withdraw", 3. Enter amount, 4. Choose withdrawal method (bank transfer, PayPal, etc.), 5. Confirm details, 6. Receive funds within 2-5 business days.

## Quality and Customer Service

### Q21: How do I handle quality issues?
**A:** Investigate the issue thoroughly, communicate with buyer promptly, propose solution (replacement, refund, compensation), resolve within 7 days, and document everything for platform records.

### Q22: How do I maintain product quality?
**A:** 1. Implement Quality Management System (ISO 9001), 2. Conduct regular inspections, 3. Test raw materials, 4. Monitor production process, 5. Pre-shipment inspection, 6. Use third-party QC, 7. Maintain quality records.

### Q23: What are the expectations for customer service?
**A:** Required: 24-hour response time, Professional communication, Accurate order processing, Proper documentation, Good packaging, Timely shipping, and Post-delivery support.

## Certification and Compliance

### Q24: What certifications should I have?
**A:** Essential: ISO 9001 (Quality Management), ISO 14001 (Environmental Management), ISO 45001 (Occupational Health & Safety), Product-specific: CE (Europe), UL (US), FCC (Electronics), GMP (Pharma), and FSSAI (Food products).

### Q25: How do I get ISO certification?
**A:** 1. Implement quality management system, 2. Choose certification body, 3. Submit application, 4. Stage 1 audit, 5. Stage 2 audit, 6. Certification awarded, 7. Maintain annual surveillance audits.

### Q26: What are the export documentation requirements?
**A:** Required documents: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading/Air Waybill, Certificate of Origin, GSP Certificate (if applicable), Export Declaration, Shipping Bill, and any product-specific licenses.

### Q27: How do I obtain a Certificate of Origin?
**A:** Contact your local Chamber of Commerce or Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) office. Submit application with commercial invoice, shipping bill, and other required documents.

## Logistics and Shipping

### Q28: How do I arrange shipping for international orders?
**A:** Options: Use the platform's logistics partners, Hire a freight forwarder, Use your own shipping arrangements, or Offer DDP shipping (you handle everything).

### Q29: What are the shipping costs?
**A:** Costs depend on: Weight and volume, Shipping method, Distance, Insurance, and Handling fees. Provide accurate shipping quotes to buyers.

### Q30: How do I handle customs clearance?
**A:** Ensure proper documentation, provide accurate HS codes, declare correct values, maintain proper packaging, and cooperate with customs authorities.

## Dispute Resolution

### Q31: How are disputes handled?
**A:** 1. Communicate with buyer directly, 2. If unresolved, platform mediates, 3. Provide all evidence, 4. Platform investigates, 5. Decision: Rule in favor of buyer, seller, or compensation, 6. Both parties must accept resolution.

### Q32: How do I prevent disputes?
**A:** 1. Accurate product descriptions, 2. Clear communication, 3. Send samples before bulk, 4. Maintain quality, 5. Use proper packaging, 6. Ship on time, 7. Provide tracking, 8. Be responsive.

### Q33: What if the buyer files a false claim?
**A:** 1. Respond promptly, 2. Provide all evidence, 3. Show documentation, 4. Contact platform support, 5. Escalate if needed, 6. Platform will investigate and rule fairly.
