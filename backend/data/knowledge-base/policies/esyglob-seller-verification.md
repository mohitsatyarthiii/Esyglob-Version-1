# EsyGlob Seller Verification Policy

## Overview

Seller verification is essential for maintaining trust and security on EsyGlob. This policy outlines the verification requirements for all sellers on the platform.

## Why Verification Matters

- **Trust Building**: Verified sellers build buyer confidence
- **Fraud Prevention**: Prevent fraudulent activities
- **Legal Compliance**: Meet regulatory requirements
- **Quality Assurance**: Ensure business legitimacy
- **Reputation Protection**: Protect platform reputation

## Verification Levels

### Level 1: Basic Verification
**Requirements:**
- Email verification
- Phone number verification
- Basic business information
- Business registration (GST/Company registration)

**Benefits:**
- Basic account functionality
- Limited listing capacity (50 products)
- Standard transaction limits ($5,000 per month)
- Standard withdrawal limits ($5,000 per month)

### Level 2: Standard Verification
**Requirements:**
- All Level 1 requirements
- Business license/registration (verified)
- Tax identification (PAN/TAN/VAT)
- Bank account verification
- Business address verification
- Business website (optional)

**Benefits:**
- Enhanced account functionality
- Increased listing capacity (200 products)
- Higher transaction limits ($25,000 per month)
- Higher withdrawal limits ($25,000 per month)
- "Verified Seller" badge
- Priority in search results

### Level 3: Premium Verification
**Requirements:**
- All Level 2 requirements
- Factory audit (physical or virtual)
- ISO certification or equivalent
- Quality management system
- Export experience documentation
- Trade reference letters
- Social compliance audit
- Sustainability credentials

**Benefits:**
- Full platform access
- Unlimited listings
- Unlimited transaction limits
- Unlimited withdrawal limits
- "Premium Verified Seller" badge
- Featured in search results
- Access to premium buyer segments
- Dedicated account manager
- Priority support

## Verification Process

### Step 1: Submit Application
- Log in to EsyGlob account
- Go to "Verification" section
- Select desired verification level
- Upload required documents
- Submit application

### Step 2: Document Review
- Document verification (1-2 days)
- Business verification (3-5 days)
- Bank account verification (1-3 days)
- Additional verification (as needed)

### Step 3: Assessment
- Business legitimacy check
- Financial health check
- Compliance check
- Quality assessment
- Background check

### Step 4: Approval/Rejection
- **Approval**: Account upgraded with badge
- **Rejection**: Reason provided; can reapply
- **Conditional Approval**: Additional steps required

### Step 5: Ongoing Verification
- Annual renewal (every 12 months)
- Document update (as documents expire)
- Random audits (as needed)
- Dispute-based verification (if issues occur)

## Seller Obligations

### During Verification
- Provide accurate and complete information
- Submit genuine documents
- Respond to verification team queries
- Complete verification within 30 days
- Pay any applicable fees

### After Verification
- Keep information updated
- Renew before expiry
- Maintain compliance
- Cooperate with random audits
- Report changes in business status

## Consequences of Non-Compliance

### Minor Violations
- **Warning**: First violation
- **Temporary Suspension**: 7 days
- **Verification Downgrade**: Level 3 → Level 2

### Major Violations
- **Permanent Suspension**: Account blocked
- **Loss of Badge**: Verification revoked
- **Public Disclosure**: Sharing of violations
- **Legal Action**: If laws violated

## Appeals Process

### How to Appeal
1. **Review Decision**: Understand the reason
2. **Gather Evidence**: Collect supporting documents
3. **File Appeal**: Submit through platform
4. **Appeal Review**: Dedicated appeals team
5. **Decision**: Final decision communicated

### Appeal Timeframe
- **Submission**: Within 14 days
- **Review**: 7-14 business days
- **Decision**: Communicated within 30 days

## Dispute Resolution

### Verification Disputes
- **Document Rejection**: Submit alternative documents
- **Verification Denied**: Request re-evaluation
- **Downgrade Decision**: Appeal process
- **Fraud Accusations**: Provide explanation and evidence
