# EsyGlob Privacy Policy

## Overview

EsyGlob is committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our B2B marketplace platform.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Name, email, phone number, business address
- **Business Details**: Company name, registration number, tax ID
- **Financial Information**: Payment details, bank account information
- **Verification Documents**: ID proofs, business licenses, certifications
- **Communication**: Messages, emails, chat history
- **Profile Information**: Photos, business descriptions, product listings

### 2. Information We Automatically Collect
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features used, time spent
- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, operating system
- **Location Data**: Approximate geographic location
- **Cookies**: Session data, preferences, analytics
- **Log Data**: Access times, pages viewed, search queries

### 3. Information from Third Parties
- **Verification Services**: Identity and business verification
- **Payment Processors**: Transaction information
- **Social Media**: If you connect through social accounts
- **Shipping Partners**: Delivery and tracking information

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses
- **Platform Operation**: Create and manage accounts, process transactions
- **Communication**: Send notifications, updates, and marketing materials
- **Verification**: Verify identity and business legitimacy
- **Fraud Prevention**: Detect and prevent fraudulent activities
- **Improvement**: Enhance platform features and user experience
- **Analytics**: Understand user behavior and preferences
- **Legal Compliance**: Meet regulatory and legal requirements

### Marketing Communications
- **Newsletters**: Platform updates and offers
- **Promotional Emails**: New features and services
- **Targeted Advertising**: Personalized content
- **Opt-Out**: You can unsubscribe anytime

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

### When We Share Information

| Recipient | Purpose | Data Shared |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Buyers/Sellers** | Facilitate transactions | Contact details, business info |
| **Payment Processors** | Process payments | Financial information |
| **Logistics Partners** | Ship orders | Address, contact details |
| **Verification Services** | Verify identity | ID documents, business info |
| **Government Agencies** | Legal compliance | Required disclosures |
| **Analytics Providers** | Platform improvement | Usage data, anonymized data |
| **Legal Authorities** | Legal requirements | As required by law |

### Third-Party Partners
- **Stripe**: Payment processing
- **Razorpay**: Indian payment gateway
- **DHL/FedEx**: Shipping and logistics
- **AWS**: Cloud hosting and infrastructure
- **Google Analytics**: Website analytics
- **SendGrid**: Email communications

## Your Rights and Choices

### Your Rights
- **Access**: Request access to your personal data
- **Correction**: Correct inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data
- **Portability**: Receive your data in portable format
- **Objection**: Object to data processing
- **Restriction**: Restrict processing in certain cases
- **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent anytime

### How to Exercise Your Rights
1. Login to your EsyGlob account
2. Go to **Account Settings > Privacy**
3. Submit your request
4. We'll respond within 30 days

## Data Security

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: AES-256 for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for transmission
- **Access Control**: Role-based access to data
- **Monitoring**: Real-time security monitoring
- **Backup**: Regular encrypted backups
- **Incident Response**: Dedicated incident response team
- **Audits**: Regular security audits and penetration testing

### Data Retention
- **Account Data**: Kept while account is active
- **Transaction Data**: 7 years for tax and legal compliance
- **Log Data**: 12 months for security and analytics
- **Marketing Data**: Until opt-out or account closure

## International Data Transfers

### Data Storage Location
- **Primary Storage**: India (Mumbai data center)
- **Backup Storage**: Singapore (Disaster recovery)
- **Processing Locations**: USA, EU, Asia-Pacific

### Compliance with International Laws
- **GDPR Compliance**: For EU users
- **CCPA Compliance**: For California residents
- **PDPB Compliance**: For Indian users (proposed)
- **Cross-Border Transfers**: Standard Contractual Clauses

## Children's Privacy

- **Age Restriction**: Platform for 18+ years only
- **No Child Data**: Not knowingly collect data from minors
- **Reporting**: Report if minor's data submitted
