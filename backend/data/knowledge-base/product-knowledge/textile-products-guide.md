# Textile Products - Complete B2B Product Guide

## Overview

This guide provides comprehensive information about textile products, including fibers, fabrics, and finished garments.

## Textile Fiber Types

### Natural Fibers

| Fiber | Source | Characteristics | Applications |
|-------|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Cotton** | Plant | Soft, absorbent | Apparel, home textiles |
| **Wool** | Animal | Warm, elastic | Winter wear, suits |
| **Silk** | Insect | Luxurious, strong | Premium apparel |
| **Linen** | Plant | Strong, cool | Summer wear, suits |
| **Jute** | Plant | Rough, strong | Bags, ropes |
| **Hemp** | Plant | Durable, sustainable | Apparel, industrial |

### Synthetic Fibers

| Fiber | Source | Characteristics | Applications |
|-------|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Polyester** | Chemical | Strong, wrinkle-resistant | Apparel, industrial |
| **Nylon** | Chemical | Strong, elastic | Activewear, stockings |
| **Acrylic** | Chemical | Warm, soft | Knitwear, blankets |
| **Spandex** | Chemical | Highly elastic | Sportswear |
| **Rayon** | Chemical | Soft, drapable | Apparel, linings |

### Blends

| Blend | Composition | Characteristics |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Cotton-Polyester** | 65/35 | Durable, easy-care |
| **Wool-Polyester** | 55/45 | Strong, wrinkle-resistant |
| **Cotton-Spandex** | 95/5 | Stretch comfort |

### Woven Fabrics

| Type | Weave | Characteristics | Applications |
|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Plain** | Simple | Strong, versatile | Shirts, bedsheets |
| **Twill** | Diagonal pattern | Durable, drapes well | Denim, suits |
| **Satin** | Float weave | Smooth, shiny | Formal wear, linings |
| **Jacquard** | Patterned | Decorative | Furnishings |
| **Damask** | Patterned | Elegant | Table linen |
| **Herringbone** | Zig-zag | Stylish | Suits, jackets |
