# Steel Products - Complete B2B Product Guide

## Overview

This guide provides comprehensive information about steel products, including grades, specifications, applications, and quality standards.

## Steel Product Categories

### 1. Flat Steel Products

#### Hot Rolled Coils (HRC)

**Description**: Steel rolled at high temperature (above recrystallization temperature)

**Specifications**:

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| **Thickness** | 1.0 - 25 mm |
| **Width** | 600 - 2500 mm |
| **Coil Weight** | 5 - 30 MT |

**Grades**:

| Grade | Yield Strength | Tensile Strength | Application |
|-------|----------------|------------------|-------------|
| **Q195** | 195 MPa | 315-430 MPa | Simple structures |
| **Q235** | 235 MPa | 370-500 MPa | General construction |
| **Q345** | 345 MPa | 470-630 MPa | Structural applications |
| **S275** | 275 MPa | 410-560 MPa | European standard |
| **S355** | 355 MPa | 470-630 MPa | High-strength |

**Applications**:
- Pipes and tubes
- Storage tanks
- Structural components
- Automotive parts
- Ship building

---

#### Cold Rolled Coils (CRC)

**Description**: Steel rolled at room temperature (after pickling)

**Specifications**:

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| **Thickness** | 0.15 - 4.0 mm |
| **Width** | 600 - 2000 mm |
| **Coil Weight** | 3 - 25 MT |

**Grades**:

| Grade | Application |
|-------|-------------|
| **SPCC** | General use |
| **SPCD** | Drawing quality |
| **SPCE** | Deep drawing |
| **DC01** | General use (European) |
| **DC03** | Drawing quality (European) |

**Applications**:
- Automotive body panels
- Household appliances
- Office furniture
- Lighting fixtures
- Tubes and pipes

---

#### Galvanized Steel

**Description**: Steel coated with zinc for corrosion protection

**Types**:

| Type | Description | Coating Weight |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| **GI (Galvanized Iron)** | Zinc coating | 40-350 g/m² |
| **GA (Galvanneal)** | Zinc-iron alloy | 40-120 g/m² |
| **GL (Galvalume)** | Aluminum-zinc | 150-200 g/m² |

**Grades**:

| Grade | Application |
|-------|-------------|
| **SGCC** | General use |
| **SGCD** | Drawing quality |
| **SGCH** | Structural use |
| **DX51D** | European standard |

**Applications**:
- Roofing sheets
- Automotive parts
- HVAC ducts
- Appliances
- Storage tanks

---

#### Pre-Painted Steel

**Description**: Galvanized steel with paint coating

**Features**:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Coating** | Polyester, PVDF, etc. |
| **Colors** | Various RAL colors |
| **Thickness** | 0.2 - 1.5 mm |
| **Width** | 600 - 1500 mm |

**Applications**:
- Building panels
- Appliances
- Furniture
- Automotive
- Signage

---

### 2. Long Steel Products

#### Rebar (TMT Bars)

**Description**: Thermo-Mechanically Treated bars for concrete reinforcement

**Specifications**:

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| **Diameter** | 8 - 40 mm |
| **Length** | 12 m (standard) |
| **Grade** | Fe 415, Fe 500, Fe 550 |

**Grades**:

| Grade | Yield Strength | Application |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| **Fe 415** | 415 MPa | General construction |
| **Fe 500** | 500 MPa | High-strength structures |
| **Fe 550** | 550 MPa | High-rise buildings |
| **Fe 600** | 600 MPa | Earthquake-resistant |

**Standards**:

| Standard | Region |
|----------|--------|
| **IS 1786** | India |
| **ASTM A615** | USA |
| **EN 10080** | Europe |
| **GB 1499** | China |

---

#### Structural Steel

**Description**: Steel sections for structural applications

**Types**:

| Type | Description | Applications |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **I-Beams** | I-shaped sections | Building frames |
| **H-Beams** | H-shaped sections | Heavy structures |
| **Channels** | C-shaped sections | Framing |
| **Angles** | L-shaped sections | Structural supports |
| **Hollow Sections** | Square/rectangular tubes | Columns, beams |

**Specifications**:

| Grade | Yield Strength | Application |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| **A36** | 250 MPa | General structures |
| **A572** | 345 MPa | High-strength |
| **A992** | 345 MPa | W-shapes |

---

#### Wire Rods

**Description**: Coiled wire for further processing

**Specifications**:

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| **Diameter** | 5.5 - 32 mm |
| **Weight** | 1 - 2 MT per coil |

**Applications**:
- Wire drawing
- Nails, screws
- Fencing
- Concrete reinforcement
- Wire mesh

---

### 3. Stainless Steel

#### Grades

| Grade | Composition | Application |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **304** | 18% Cr, 8% Ni | General use |
| **316** | 16% Cr, 10% Ni, 2% Mo | Marine, chemical |
| **201** | 16% Cr, 4% Ni | Low cost alternative |
| **409** | 11% Cr | Automotive |
| **430** | 16% Cr | Appliances |

**Forms**:
- Sheets and plates
- Coils
- Bars
- Tubes and pipes
- Fittings

**Applications**:
- Kitchenware
- Food processing
- Medical equipment
- Chemical plants
- Marine applications
