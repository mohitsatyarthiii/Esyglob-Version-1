# How to Register on EsyGlob - Step by Step Tutorial

## Overview

This tutorial will guide you through the complete registration process on EsyGlob B2B marketplace platform.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:

- ✅ Business email address
- ✅ Business registration documents
- ✅ Tax ID / GST number
- ✅ Bank account details
- ✅ Government-issued ID (Passport, Aadhaar, etc.)

## Step 1: Visit EsyGlob Website

**Action**: Open your browser and go to esyglob.com

**Tips**:
- Use Google Chrome, Firefox, or Edge for best experience
- Clear browser cache if you've visited before
- Ensure stable internet connection

---

## Step 2: Click Sign Up

**Action**: Click the "Sign Up" button on the top right corner


**Tips**:
- Look for the blue button
- Desktop and mobile versions have similar layout

---

## Step 3: Choose Your Role

**Action**: Select your role as either "Buyer" or "Seller"

### For Buyers:
- ✅ I want to purchase products
- ✅ I'm looking for suppliers
- ✅ I want to import goods

### For Sellers:
- ✅ I want to sell products
- ✅ I'm looking for buyers
- ✅ I want to export goods

**Tips**:
- You can't change this easily later
- Choose based on your primary business activity

---

## Step 4: Fill Basic Information

**Action**: Complete the registration form

**Required Fields**:

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Email** | Business email | info@yourcompany.com |
| **Password** | Strong password | Your@1Secure2Pass |
| **Confirm Password** | Re-enter password | Your@1Secure2Pass |
| **Business Name** | Registered business name | Your Company Pvt Ltd |
| **Contact Person** | Your full name | John Smith |
| **Phone Number** | With country code | +91-9876543210 |
| **Country** | Your business country | India |

**Password Requirements**:
- ✅ Minimum 8 characters
- ✅ At least one uppercase letter
- ✅ At least one lowercase letter
- ✅ At least one number
- ✅ At least one special character

**Tips**:
- Use professional email (not personal Gmail)
- Save your password securely
- Use a password manager

---

## Step 5: Verify Email Address

**Action**: Check your email for verification link

**Verification Email**:

From: EsyGlob noreply@esyglob.com
Subject: Verify Your Email Address
Content: Click the link to verify your email


**Process**:
1. Open your email inbox
2. Find EsyGlob verification email
3. Click the verification link
4. You'll be redirected to EsyGlob

**Troubleshooting**:
- Check spam/junk folder
- Click "Resend Verification" if not received
- Wait 5-10 minutes before resending

---

## Step 6: Complete Profile

**Action**: Fill in your complete business profile

### Company Details

| Field | Description | Required |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Company Name** | Legal business name | Yes |
| **Registration Number** | Business registration ID | Yes |
| **GST/Tax ID** | Tax identification number | Yes |
| **Business Type** | Manufacturer/Trader/Exporter | Yes |
| **Established Year** | Year of incorporation | Yes |
| **Number of Employees** | Staff count | Optional |
| **Annual Turnover** | Revenue range | Optional |

### Address Details

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Street Address** | Full street address |
| **City** | City name |
| **State/Province** | State or province |
| **Postal Code** | Zip/Pin code |
| **Country** | Country of operation |

### Contact Details

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Phone Number** | Primary contact number |
| **Alternate Phone** | Secondary number |
| **Website** | Company website |
| **Social Media** | LinkedIn, Twitter, etc. |

### Business Description

**What to Include**:
1. **Overview**: What your business does
2. **Products/Services**: What you offer
3. **Specialization**: Your expertise
4. **Target Markets**: Who you serve
5. **Achievements**: Awards, certifications

**Example Description**:

We are a leading manufacturer of industrial steel products
with over 20 years of experience. We specialize in hot-rolled
steel coils and export to 15+ countries. We are ISO 9001 and
ISO 14001 certified. Our annual production capacity is 500,000
tonnes.


**Character Limit**: 500-1000 words

## Step 7: Upload Documents

**Action**: Upload required verification documents

### For All Users

| Document | Format | Size Limit |
|----------|--------|------------|
| **Business Registration** | PDF/JPG | 5 MB |
| **Tax ID / GST Certificate** | PDF/JPG | 5 MB |
| **Director/Owner ID** | PDF/JPG | 5 MB |
| **Bank Details** | PDF/JPG | 5 MB |

### Additional for Sellers

| Document | Format | Size Limit |
|----------|--------|------------|
| **Import Export Code** | PDF/JPG | 5 MB |
| **Product Certifications** | PDF/JPG | 5 MB |
| **Business License** | PDF/JPG | 5 MB |

**Document Tips**:
- Use clear, scanned copies
- All documents must be valid
- Keep original documents ready
- Acceptable formats: PDF, JPG, PNG

---

## Step 8: Verification Process

**Action**: Wait for verification completion

### Verification Timeline

| Verification Level | Timeframe |
|-------------------|-----------|
| **Basic** | 24-48 hours |
| **Standard** | 3-5 business days |
| **Premium** | 5-10 business days |

### What Happens During Verification

1. **Document Review**: Documents checked for authenticity
2. **Business Validation**: Business existence verified
3. **Background Check**: No adverse records
4. **Approval**: Account activated

### Status Updates

You'll receive:
- Email updates on progress
- Dashboard notification
- SMS alerts (if enabled)

---

## Step 9: Dashboard Setup

**Action**: Set up your dashboard preferences

### For Buyers

1. **Set Preferred Currency**: USD, EUR, INR, etc.
2. **Configure Notifications**: Email/SMS preferences
3. **Save Favorite Suppliers**: Quick access
4. **Set Up Budget Alerts**: Spending limits

### For Sellers

1. **Store Setup**: Add logo, banner, store description
2. **Payment Settings**: Bank account, PayPal, etc.
3. **Shipping Settings**: Configure shipping methods
4. **Product Categories**: Select your categories
5. **Notification Preferences**: Order alerts, inquiries

---

## Step 10: Start Using EsyGlob

### For Buyers

1. **Search Products**: Use search bar
2. **Request Quotes**: Send inquiries
3. **Order Samples**: Test before bulk
4. **Place Orders**: Make purchases
5. **Manage Transactions**: Track orders

### For Sellers

1. **List Products**: Add your products
2. **Respond to Inquiries**: Answer buyer questions
3. **Manage Orders**: Process orders
4. **Track Sales**: Monitor performance
5. **Get Paid**: Receive payments
